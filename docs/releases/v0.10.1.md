# The Scathing Charter v0.10.1 (beta)

A bigger card hearing with all-new card pictures, smoother play, handier trading and, for the first time, music.

## Play

- **On Windows:** download **ScathingCharter-Setup-0.10.1.exe** below and run it to install, or take **ScathingCharter-Portable-0.10.1.exe** to play without installing. The game isn't signed, so Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It needs nothing else: no Node.js, no browser, no internet.
- **In your browser:** https://ullafreehum.github.io/scathing-charter/

First game? Pick **Easy (Fair Terms)**. Updating from an older version? Your saves carry on.

## What's new

- **A bigger hearing**: the card duel fills the screen, with new paintings on all 30 cards and a new card frame. Hover a card in your hand to see it full size.
- **Music**: *Ash & Ebony*, a score that darkens when Baar Dau falls and quickens in a duel. Turn it on in Settings → Sound → Music.
- **Shift for max**: hold Shift in the market and the Buy and Sell of the row under your cursor become Buy max and Sell max.
- **Interest at a glance**: hover the Bank figure in the top bar, or a strongbox, to see its rate, what your money earns a day and what it has earned so far.
- **Quick, readable hover tips** everywhere, and smoother trading in the browser.
- **An Exit button** on the title screen.
- **Economy**: black-market sales of restricted goods drop the price faster, informers raise your heat, flooded markets recover sooner, and the Scathing Charter now costs 90,000.
- **Art of the Red Year**, a short art book, is in the repository (docs/artbook).
