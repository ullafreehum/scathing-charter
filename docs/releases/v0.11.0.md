# The Scathing Charter v0.11.0

The game feels like a game: quick, quiet motion, sounds under your fingers, two big moments, and a card duel with weight.

## Play

- **On Windows:** download **ScathingCharter-Setup-0.11.0.exe** below and run it to install, or take **ScathingCharter-Portable-0.11.0.exe** to play without installing. The game isn't signed, so Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **On Android:** download **ScathingCharter-0.11.0-Android.apk** below and open it on your phone. Android will ask you to allow installs from unknown sources. Hold the phone sideways.
- **In your browser:** https://ullafreehum.github.io/scathing-charter/

First game? Pick **Easy (Fair Terms)**. Updating? Your saves carry on.

## What's new

- **Everything moves the same short way.** Tabs and screens fade, sheets slide in from their edge, and nothing bounces. Buttons sink when you press them.
- **Numbers count.** Cash, debt and cargo count to their new values, and the top bar stays still.
- **A day passing has a beat.** The screen dims, the date turns like a page, then your debt and cash count.
- **The fall of Baar Dau.** The screen cuts to black, the boom lands, the game shakes once, then the news arrives.
- **Quiet sounds** for presses, tabs, opening and closing, finished trades and refusals. On the phone, trades and refusals buzz.
- **The card duel has weight.** Normal is now a real pace. Your card arcs in and its damage lands a beat later, and the rival's card turns over before it plays. The hand is fanned, and the duel has its own sounds. **Speed** still offers Fast and Skip.
- **Settings:** "Animations" is now **Reduce motion**, and the game no longer looks like a web page.
