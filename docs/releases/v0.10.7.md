# The Scathing Charter v0.10.7 (beta)

A small fix release after 0.10.6, the trader's voice.

## Play

- **On Windows:** download **ScathingCharter-Setup-0.10.7.exe** below and run it to install, or take **ScathingCharter-Portable-0.10.7.exe** to play without installing. The game isn't signed, so Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It needs nothing else: no Node.js, no browser, no internet.
- **On Android (test build):** download **ScathingCharter-0.10.7-Android-A26-debug.apk** below and open it on your phone. Android will ask you to allow installs from unknown sources. It is made for the Samsung Galaxy A26 held sideways; other phones may work but aren't tested.
- **In your browser:** https://ullafreehum.github.io/scathing-charter/

First game? Pick **Easy (Fair Terms)**. Updating from an older version? Your saves carry on.

## What's new

Three small fixes to 0.10.6:

- Ald'ruhn no longer shows a "masons are rebuilding" paragraph on day 1.
- Gavas Drelloth counts a payment made on day 0 as paid.
- The end screen's look back at the eruption is covered by more tests (cargo burned in Balmora, Ald’ruhn and Sadrith Mora).

Saves from older versions load as before.

Full list: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/ullafreehum/scathing-charter/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
