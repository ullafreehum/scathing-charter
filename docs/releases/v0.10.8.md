# The Scathing Charter v0.10.8

The first full release: the Android app now fits every common phone, and the game is no longer marked beta.

## Play

- **On Windows:** download **ScathingCharter-Setup-0.10.8.exe** below and run it to install, or take **ScathingCharter-Portable-0.10.8.exe** to play without installing. The game isn't signed, so Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **On Android:** download **ScathingCharter-0.10.8-Android.apk** below and open it on your phone. Android will ask you to allow installs from unknown sources. Hold the phone sideways.
- **In your browser:** https://ullafreehum.github.io/scathing-charter/

First game? Pick **Easy (Fair Terms)**. Updating? Your saves carry on.

## What's new

- **Any phone, held sideways.** The Android app was made for the Galaxy A26; it now fits every common phone from small (640×360) to large (932×430). On smaller phones the top strip keeps your date, town, money and cargo, and Drelloth's line stays on the Bank tab.
- **The buzz works.** The short buzz on a card's outcome, and the longer one when Baar Dau falls, now reach the phone.
- **Everything from 0.10.6 and 0.10.7:** your Journal in your own voice, merchants and Gavas Drelloth who remember you, ruined towns that look ruined, the end screen's look back at the eruption, and separate switches for sound effects and music.
