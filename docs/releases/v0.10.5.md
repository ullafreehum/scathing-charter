# The Scathing Charter v0.10.5 (beta)

More music, and a first Android test app.

## Play

- **On Windows:** download **ScathingCharter-Setup-0.10.5.exe** below and run it to install, or take **ScathingCharter-Portable-0.10.5.exe** to play without installing. The game isn't signed, so Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It needs nothing else: no Node.js, no browser, no internet.
- **On Android (test build):** download **ScathingCharter-0.10.5-Android-A26-debug.apk** below and open it on your phone. Android will ask you to allow installs from unknown sources. It is made for the Samsung Galaxy A26 held sideways; other phones may work but aren't tested.
- **In your browser:** https://ullafreehum.github.io/scathing-charter/

First game? Pick **Easy (Fair Terms)**. Updating from an older version? Your saves carry on.

## What's new

- **Three more songs**: Salt & Silt, Red Mountain Procession and Ghostgate Vigil join Ash & Ebony. With Music on (Settings), the game moves to another song every five minutes and keeps the mood: calm, after Baar Dau, or the hearing.
- **The Android test app**, laid out for a phone held sideways:
  - The tabs sit in a rail on the left, and your date, town, cash, debt and cargo sit in a strip along the top. Tap the strip for the town's news, your money or the menu.
  - In the Market, the buttons say what a tap does ("Buy 5", "Sell all 6") or why they can't act. Tap a row for its details, and hold a Buy or Sell button for the most you can trade on that row. An Undo bar follows every trade.
  - The map fills the screen: pinch or double-tap to zoom, tap a town to see the ways there and Go, and use Places for a list.
  - The Back button closes what is open, then goes back a tab, then asks before quitting to the title. Music pauses when you leave the app.
  - There is no card duel on the phone: fines and seized goods stand, with no hearing. Saves stay on the phone (no file import or export).
- Everything from 0.10.2: the Solstheim ebony and stalhrim fix, dearer restricted goods, the bigger card hearing, Shift for Buy max and Sell max, bank interest tips and smoother play.

Desktop and browser play are unchanged.
