# The Scathing Charter v0.10.2 (beta)

A balance fix: the Solstheim ebony and stalhrim route no longer breaks the economy.

## Play

- **On Windows:** download **ScathingCharter-Setup-0.10.2.exe** below and run it to install, or take **ScathingCharter-Portable-0.10.2.exe** to play without installing. The game isn't signed, so Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It needs nothing else: no Node.js, no browser, no internet.
- **In your browser:** https://ullafreehum.github.io/scathing-charter/

First game? Pick **Easy (Fair Terms)**. Updating from an older version? Your saves carry on.

## What's new

- **The Solstheim fix**: ebony and stalhrim no longer make an easy fortune between Raven Rock and Skyrim. Raven Rock sells them dearer, and Skyrim and the Imperial City pay less for them.
- **Restricted goods** cost more to buy (half again from the East Empire Company, 40% more from a fence), and buying many at once raises their price faster.
- Everything from 0.10.1: the bigger card hearing with new pictures, music, Shift for Buy max and Sell max, interest tips and smoother play.

Full list: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/ullafreehum/scathing-charter/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
