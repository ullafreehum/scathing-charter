# The Scathing Charter v0.10.9 (pre-release)

The magistrate's card duel comes to the Android app, and the game gets its own icon.

## Play

- **On Windows:** download **ScathingCharter-Setup-0.10.9.exe** below and run it to install, or take **ScathingCharter-Portable-0.10.9.exe** to play without installing. The game isn't signed, so Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **On Android:** download **ScathingCharter-0.10.9-Android.apk** below and open it on your phone. Android will ask you to allow installs from unknown sources. Hold the phone sideways.
- **In your browser:** https://ullafreehum.github.io/scathing-charter/

First game? Pick **Easy (Fair Terms)**. Updating? Your saves carry on.

## What's new

- **The card duel on the phone.** Contest a fine or a seizure from the Market, or play a practice duel from the title screen. The duel fills the screen: tap a card to see it big, then tap **Play** (or the card again). **Discard**, **Speed** and **Rules** sit beside the towers, and your hand has bigger type.
- **A new icon:** a sealed charter, on Android and on Windows.
