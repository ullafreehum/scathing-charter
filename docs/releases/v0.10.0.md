# The Scathing Charter v0.10.0 (beta)

It is 1 Morning Star 4E 4 in Balmora. You owe the Camonna Tong 4,000 septims and you have three years: trade your way out of debt, then buy a seat in the Scathing Charter before the end of 4E 6. This is the focus release: one clear goal, one choice per trip, and a Red Year you can see on the map.

## Play

- **On Windows:** download **ScathingCharter-Setup-0.10.0.exe** below and run it to install, or take **ScathingCharter-Portable-0.10.0.exe** to play without installing. The game isn't signed, so Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It needs nothing else: no Node.js, no browser, no internet.
- **In your browser:** https://ullafreehum.github.io/scathing-charter/

First game? Pick **Easy (Fair Terms)**. Updating from an older version? Your saves carry on.

## What's new

- **A clear goal**, always in the top bar: pay off the Tong, then buy the Scathing Charter. Holding it with no debt wins.
- **The magistrate's card hearing**: contest seizures and black-market fines in a 30-card tower duel, in the town where it happened or at the courts of Vivec, Ebonheart, Narsis and Mournhold. It's always your choice. You can also play the duel on its own from the title screen.
- **Crossing cards** on the road, at most one per journey, with the odds and stakes of every choice printed.
- **The Red Year on the map**: ash haze, the eruption, closed lines and refugee camps, with paintings for the great events.
- **One strongbox per bank city**, a Charter in three tiers, and a debt that can't grow past six times the note before the Tong marks you.
- **The Tong comes for you** on the road, and now in town too if you rest with the note overdue.
- **A Windows app** that opens full screen and fits any screen from 1024×600 to 4K.
- Balance from many simulated playthroughs, faster browser play, and many fixes from playtesting.
