# The Scathing Charter v0.10.6 (beta)

The trader's voice: your Ledger, the merchants and the Tong now talk to you, and the Android app gets a round of polish.

## Play

- **On Windows:** download **ScathingCharter-Setup-0.10.6.exe** below and run it to install, or take **ScathingCharter-Portable-0.10.6.exe** to play without installing. The game isn't signed, so Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It needs nothing else: no Node.js, no browser, no internet.
- **On Android (test build):** download **ScathingCharter-0.10.6-Android-A26-debug.apk** below and open it on your phone. Android will ask you to allow installs from unknown sources. It is made for the Samsung Galaxy A26 held sideways; other phones may work but aren't tested.
- **In your browser:** https://ullafreehum.github.io/scathing-charter/

First game? Pick **Easy (Fair Terms)**. Updating from an older version? Your saves carry on.

## What's new

- **Your Ledger speaks in your own voice**: hopeful and naive before Baar Dau falls, sober after it.
- **What you knew, and when**: the end screen says how often you were warned about the eruption and what you left in the vaults when it fell.
- **Merchants remember you**: they speak differently to a saint, to a profiteer, in a flooded market and to a regular.
- **Gavas Drelloth** has his say on the Bank tab, by how hard the Tong is pressing and how you have paid.
- **Your background shows**: five Chronicle cards carry a line only your character would feel.
- **Ruined towns feel ruined**: a dated paragraph above the market tells you what is left of the town.
- **Sound effects and Music** now have separate switches, and there is a new app icon.
- **Android polish**:
  - Five Market rows fit on screen. Goods you don't carry show no Sell button.
  - The hint goes after your first sale, and a town notice opens once and then folds to one line.
  - The top strip shows Drelloth's short line while you owe the Tong.
  - Pop-ups go away sooner, tapping the date shows the time, and a short buzz marks the outcome of a card (a longer one when Baar Dau falls).
  - The app keeps your tab, open sheet and Market position when you come back to it.

Saves from older versions load as before.
