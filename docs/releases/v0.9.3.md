**The Scathing Charter** is a free, unofficial trading game set in Morrowind at the dawn of the Fourth Era. You
owe the Camonna Tong, the Red Year is coming, and history happens on real dates. This is the **first public beta**.

## Download and play (Windows, offline)
1. Install **Node.js** (LTS, version 20 or newer) from https://nodejs.org and **Google Chrome**, if you don't have them.
2. Download **`AshGame-offline-v0.9.3.zip`** below.
3. Right-click it → **Extract All…** → choose `C:\Games` → **Extract**. You get `C:\Games\AshGame`.
4. Double-click **`Play The Scathing Charter.cmd`**. The game opens in its own Chrome window, even if Chrome is
   not your default browser. No internet is needed.

Updating from an earlier copy: extract over the old folder and choose **Replace**. Your saves (in
`AshGame\chrome-profile`) are kept and old saves load.

macOS / Linux: extract anywhere and run `node launcher/server.mjs`, or play from source with `./play.sh`.

## What's in it
- **The Red Year campaign** (4E 4–4E 6) and the unlockable **Long Chronicle** to the coronation of Titus Mede I.
- 20 towns, 37 routes in 9 travel modes, 16 goods, 25 dated history events, about 100 rumours, a sourced Codex
  with 24 "historians disagree" entries.
- Debt to the Camonna Tong, two banks, contracts, smuggling with **heat**, markets that remember your trades, a
  price memory of every market you've seen, and **the Scathing Charter**: trade rights to rebuild the Vivec region.
- A zoomable map with travel cards and a route planner; next-step hints; a fortune chart; a campaign timeline.
- Five readable themes from Vvardenfell's regions, four fonts, text size, sound and motion settings.

## Beta notes
- Balance is tested with bots, not yet with full human playthroughs. Known: smuggling still pays more than honest
