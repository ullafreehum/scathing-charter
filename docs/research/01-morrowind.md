# Morrowind & Vvardenfell (incl. mainland Morrowind), 3E 427 – c. 4E 40
_Researcher notes for Ashfall Ledger. Status tags: (no tag) = verified via cited source; [unverified]; [fan]; CONFLICT._

> Method note: compiled from ~36 salvaged WebSearch result summaries plus 5 follow-up searches (UESP / Imperial Library only).
> UESP mirror URLs (`content1.`, `content2.`, `en.m.`, `http://`) were normalised to `https://en.uesp.net/wiki/<Namespace>:<Page_Name>`;
> where a search returned a redirect URL (e.g. `Lore:Resdayn`, `Lore:Dark_Elf`, `Lore:Ministry_of_Truth`), the target page title is cited instead.
> In-game books (e.g. *The Red Year*, *Pocket Guide to the Empire*) are primary but are in-universe testimony; author bias is noted where relevant.
> Searches that failed (search budget exhausted): Mages Guild in 4E Morrowind, Twin Lamps. Everything on those topics below is `[unverified]`.

## 1. Timeline (dated facts, oldest → newest; focus 3E 427 – 4E 40)

### Late Third Era (background the player will hear about in rumours)
- **3E 427** — The Nerevarine's campaign ends with Dagoth Ur destroyed; the *Pocket Guide to the Empire, 3rd Ed.* says Dagoth Ur and two Tribunes, Almalexia and Sotha Sil, were "destroyed in the Nerevarine's fury", and that the Nerevarine "likewise has vanished". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Pocket_Guide_to_the_Empire,_3rd_Edition/Morrowind
- **3E 427** — Almalexia kills Sotha Sil in the Clockwork City, unleashes its fabricants on Mournhold to frame him, then dies at the Nerevarine's hand in the Clockwork City when her powers fail. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Almalexia
- **3E 427** — Vivec retrieves the bodies of Almalexia and Sotha Sil and inters them "in the proper Velothi fashion"; Azura appears in Mournhold and clears its ash storms as a "gift to the Dunmer people". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Almalexia ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mournhold
- **3E 427** — Hlaalu Helseth's reign as King of Morrowind begins (end date unrecorded: "3E 427 – 3E ?" / "4E ??"). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mournhold ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Morrowind
- **3E 427 (after the Red Mountain events)** — Mages Guild Arch-Mage of Vvardenfell Trebonius Artorius orders the deaths of the Telvanni Councilors; how most fared is unknown, but Neloth survived. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Sadrith_Mora
- **3E 427 or 3E 433 (disputed)** — Vivec is stripped of divinity and disappears (see CONFLICT §6). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Mask_of_Vivec ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Vivec
- **3E 430** — Helseth marries Lady Vendama of Bodrum Mora, binding House Hlaalu to House Dres; Houses Indoril and Redoran, objecting to the alliance and to royal overreach, rise in open rebellion against the crown. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Slavery ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hlaalu_Helseth
- **3E 430 (c. six months into the revolt)** — Helseth decrees the abolition of slavery throughout Morrowind, apparently to incite slave risings in Indoril and Redoran lands; the decree "was apparently successful in ending the practice of slavery across Morrowind". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hlaalu_Helseth ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Slavery
- **By 3E 432** — The Empire reports that Almalexia, Dagoth Ur and Sotha Sil (and possibly Vivec) have been destroyed. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Almalexia
- **3E 433 (Oblivion Crisis)** — Daedra destroy Ald'ruhn; House Redoran re-animates the Emperor Crab Ald Skar to defend it, but it also falls. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ald'ruhn
- **3E 433** — Rumours in Cyrodiil report Helseth's "unprecedented" Hlaalu–Dres alliance and the abolition of slavery; also the last rumours of Vivec's fate and that the Nerevarine sailed for Akavir. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Hlaalu ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Vivec ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Nerevarine
- **3E 433** — Morrowind's Raven Rock histories record that the bulk of the Imperial Guard on Solstheim was recalled to Cyrodiil during the Crisis (see CONFLICT on Imperial withdrawal). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Oblivion_Crisis ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:History_of_Raven_Rock

### Early Fourth Era
- **4E 0–4 (after the Crisis)** — House Hlaalu, tied to the Imperial administration, becomes the scapegoat for Dunmer suffering; Redoran becomes the leading House. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Hlaalu ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hlaalu_Helseth
- **Before 4E 5** — Telvanni wizard Neloth moves to Solstheim and grows his citadel Tel Mithryn from cuttings brought from Morrowind (see CONFLICT: Tel Mithryn founding date). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Neloth
- **Before 4E 5** — With Vivec gone, the spells holding the moonlet Baar Dau (the Ministry of Truth) dissipate; the Dunmer mage Vuhon (with Sul) builds the Ingenium, a soul-consuming device, to keep it aloft. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Baar_Dau ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ingenium
- **4E 5** — Sul tries to rescue his lover Ilzheven, whom Vuhon had fed to the Ingenium; the device loses its power source, destabilises and explodes, and the Ministry of Truth resumes its fall. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ingenium ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Books:The_Infernal_City_Plot_Summary
- **4E 5, 3 Sun's Dawn** — Baar Dau strikes Vivec City; Red Mountain erupts "only minutes later" (the "Red Year"). Date given by a single UESP page; see CONFLICT. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Year
- **4E 5, 3 Sun's Dawn** — The impact obliterates Vivec City, leaving a boiling crater later called Scathing Bay; tsunamis ravage mainland Morrowind. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Baar_Dau ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Year
- **4E 5, Sun's Dawn** — The eruption obliterates Gnisis and causes widespread destruction in Ald'ruhn, Balmora and Sadrith Mora; the Grazelands NE of the mountain are known to have survived. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Year
- **4E 5, Sun's Dawn** — Earthquakes reach the mainland city of Tear (which partially floods and sinks into its swamp); roads further south in Black Marsh are destroyed. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Year ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Tear
- **4E 5, Sun's Dawn** — Fort Frostmoth on south-eastern Solstheim is wiped out (one source: General Carius killed), leaving few Imperial guards on the island; southern Solstheim is devastated by ash. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Year ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- **4E 5** — Castle Ebonheart is *assumed* (not documented) to have been obliterated by the fall of Baar Dau because of its proximity to Vivec City. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Castle_Ebonheart
- **4E 5** — The eruption is the most powerful of Red Mountain's to date; it permanently lowers the peak, and the Throat of the World becomes Tamriel's highest mountain from then on. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Mountain
- **4E 5** — The Red Year causes "widespread death and destruction along with a mass exodus of Morrowind's inhabitants"; thousands die. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Mountain ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era
- **4E 5, c. early First Seed (≈ one month after the eruption)** — House Redoran organises relief out of Mournhold (undamaged): a Redoran councilor directs soldiers, supplies and able-bodied mer to the hardest-hit settlements such as Balmora. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Year ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:The_Red_Year,_Vol._I
- **4E 5, c. First Seed – Second Seed** — A relief worker spends "maybe two months" rebuilding Balmora; Balmora is subsequently rebuilt and re-inhabited. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Balmora ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/red-year-vol-i
- **4E 5 → later** — Tribune worship, already waning, collapses; the Tribunal Temple dissolves and is replaced by the New Temple (date of formal transition not given). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dissident_Priests ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:The_Reclamations
- **4E 5 or soon after (date uncertain)** — House Hlaalu is stripped of Great House status and its Grand Council seat; House Sadras is chosen for the fifth seat. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Hlaalu ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Sadras
- **4E 5 or 4E 6 (disputed)** — Morrowind's capital is moved permanently from Mournhold to the Redoran city of Blacklight. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Blacklight ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Year ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Redoran
- **4E 6** — The Accession War ("Argonian Invasion"): the An-Xileel of Black Marsh invade Morrowind "in the immediate aftermath of the Red Year". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era_Wars
- **4E 6 (c. one year after the relief effort began)** — Argonians sack Mournhold, the provincial capital; many other southern cities are sacked too. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mournhold ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Morrowind
- **4E 6, by mid-year** — Argonians have pushed deep into House Telvanni territory in the north-east; the House is almost completely destroyed. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War
- **4E 6** — The invasion reaches as far as the southern coast of Vvardenfell. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mournhold
- **4E 6 → ?** — A House Redoran army halts the Argonian advance; countless Dunmer are killed or displaced and found dozens of colonies abroad, including Solstheim; many flee to eastern Skyrim. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era
- **Between 4E 6 and 4E 28** — "At some point" the Argonians stop advancing; lingering skirmishes continue for centuries. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Wars ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore_talk:Accession_War
- **4E 16** — The High King of Skyrim formally gives Solstheim to Morrowind as a refuge for the Dunmer; the island passes fully to House Redoran; the Telvanni found Tel Mithryn on the south-east coast. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era
- **4E 16** — Most of Raven Rock's remaining Imperial population departs; Brara Morvayn becomes the settlement's first Councilor. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:History_of_Raven_Rock ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Brara_Morvayn
- **c. 4E 17–22** — Titus Mede I takes the throne, ending the Stormcrown Interregnum (date disputed — see CONFLICT). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mede_Dynasty
- **Decades after 4E 5** — Ash continues to poison land and sea; Red Mountain spews ash "even more regularly than before" and Vvardenfell stays largely uninhabitable. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Mountain ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Vvardenfell
- **c. 4E 40** — *The Infernal City*: Umbriel (Vuhon fused with the sword-spirit Umbra), powered by a second Ingenium, menaces Tamriel (Black Marsh first) [unverified for the year]. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Books:The_Infernal_City_Plot_Summary
- **Before 4E 48** — Morrowind ceases to be an Imperial province (exact date unknown). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mede_Dynasty
- **Later context (outside main scope)** — 4E 95: Hlaalu agents try to assassinate the Raven Rock Councilor; 4E 201: a second Hlaalu plot by Vendil (son of Vilur) is foiled by the Last Dragonborn. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Hlaalu
- **Later context** — By 4E 168 Morrowind is listed among provinces no longer in the Empire; by the 3rd century 4E the Royal Palace of Mournhold stands empty; as of 4E 201 Red Mountain is still erupting with ash reaching Solstheim. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Titus_Mede_II ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mournhold ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Mountain

## 2. Places (status in 4E 4, 4E 5, 4E 6, and later if known)

### Vivec City (Vvardenfell, south coast)
- Controlling faction / ruler: Tribunal Temple seat (dissolving after Vivec's disappearance); within the Imperial District of Vvardenfell under Duke Vedam Dren in 3E 427 [unverified for 4E 4]. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Vivec_City ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Vedam_Dren
- Economy & signature goods: pilgrimage and Temple economy; trade hub with a southern caravan road to Narsis worked by pack guar (Saldus Llervu's route). — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/red-year-vol-ii
- 4E 4: still standing beneath the hovering Ministry of Truth (Baar Dau), now held up by the Ingenium rather than Vivec's power. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Baar_Dau
- 4E 5 (3 Sun's Dawn): obliterated by Baar Dau's impact; the crater becomes the boiling waters of Scathing Bay. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Year
- Later: "an island ruin of shattered stone and ash"; it is unknown whether any relief or rebuilding effort was attempted. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Vivec_City
- Atmosphere: witnesses on the south road recall "a sudden, unnatural silence", guar going wild, then a blast from the city centre and a pillar of fire from Red Mountain minutes later. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:The_Red_Year,_Vol._II

### Ministry of Truth / Baar Dau
- Controlling faction / ruler: Tribunal Temple prison (hollowed moonlet where dissident priests were jailed for "reform"). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Baar_Dau
- 4E 4: kept aloft by the soul-eating Ingenium designed by Vuhon. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ingenium
- 4E 5: falls when the Ingenium explodes; strike triggers the Red Year. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Baar_Dau
- Sources: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Baar_Dau ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ingenium

### Red Mountain & the Vvardenfell interior
- 4E 5: catastrophic eruption; summit permanently lowered (Throat of the World now highest peak). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Mountain
- 4E 6 onward: ash smothers much of Morrowind, Vvardenfell "nearly uninhabitable"; ash poisons land and sea for decades. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Mountain
- The Grazelands (north-east of the mountain) are known to have survived the eruption. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Year
- Atmosphere: survivors were "blown off their feet"; one lay two days in a riverbed before healers found her. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:The_Red_Year,_Vol._I

### Balmora (Vvardenfell, West Gash / Odai valley)
- Controlling faction / ruler: House Hlaalu (Hlaalu council seat in 3E 427); other Hlaalu towns: Suran, Hla Oad, Gnaar Mok. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Morrowind:House_Hlaalu
- Economy & signature goods: Hlaalu trade town; Camonna Tong base; silt strider port linking Ald'ruhn, Seyda Neen, Suran and Vivec; Mages Guild Guild Guide service. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Camonna_Tong ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Morrowind:Transport
- 4E 5: devastated by the eruption ("hardly anything left in town was still standing"); relief arrives c. a month later via Redoran directives. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Balmora ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/red-year-vol-i
- 4E 5–6 and later: rebuilt and re-inhabited; still an inhabited settlement in the late Fourth Era. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Balmora
- Atmosphere: the Odai River runs through the city centre, dividing it into four districts; the river's fate after the Red Year is unknown. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Odai_River ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Morrowind:Balmora
- Hlaalu-to-Redoran shift: with Hlaalu disgraced and Redoran running the relief, a rebuilt Balmora is likely Redoran-influenced [unverified inference].

### Ald'ruhn (Vvardenfell, Ashlands)
- Controlling faction / ruler: House Redoran (Vvardenfell seat); built in the shell of the ancient Emperor Crab Skar, slain by Dranoth Hleran. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ald'ruhn ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Emperor_Crab
- 4E 4: already recovering from destruction by Daedra in 3E 433 (when the reanimated crab Ald Skar also fell). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ald'ruhn
- 4E 5: "further devastation" from the eruption; named among the hardest-hit towns in Mournhold reports on day one. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ald'ruhn ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:The_Red_Year,_Vol._II
- Later: no confirmed rebuilding [unverified].

### Gnisis (Vvardenfell, West Gash)
- 4E 5: obliterated; Neria Relethyl is the only known survivor ("when I could finally stand, Gnisis was gone"). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Gnisis ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:The_Red_Year,_Vol._I
- Later: no source on rebuilding. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Gnisis

### Sadrith Mora (Zafirbel Bay, east of Vvardenfell)
- Controlling faction / ruler: House Telvanni council town; nearby Imperial fort Wolverine Hall; Neloth's tower Tel Naga in 3E. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Sadrith_Mora ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Wolverine_Hall ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Morrowind:Tel_Naga
- 4E 4: Telvanni council thinned by Trebonius's assassination order (3E 427). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Sadrith_Mora
- 4E 5: listed as suffering "widespread destruction", but the extent is explicitly unknown. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Year ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Sadrith_Mora
- 4E 6: Telvanni lands overrun by Argonians by mid-year. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War
- Fate of Wolverine Hall and Tel Naga after 4E 5: unknown. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Wolverine_Hall

### Ebonheart & Castle Ebonheart (Vvardenfell, beside Vivec City)
- Controlling faction / ruler: Imperial administration of the Vvardenfell District (Duke Vedam Dren, ex-Hlaalu Grandmaster) [unverified for 4E 4]; East Empire Company presence [unverified]. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Vedam_Dren
- 4E 5: Castle Ebonheart presumed obliterated by the fall of Baar Dau (inference only). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Castle_Ebonheart
- Duke Vedam Dren's fate and successor: not found in canon sources. A "death of the Duke" appears only in a Tamriel Rebuilt quest [fan]. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Vedam_Dren

### Mournhold (mainland, Deshaan; royal capital)
- Controlling faction / ruler: King Hlaalu Helseth (from 3E 427), seat of the Royal Palace; Grand Council capital until 4E 5/6. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mournhold
- 4E 4: post-Almalexia city; Azura has cleared its ash storms. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mournhold
- 4E 5: spared damage; becomes the hub for Redoran relief to Vvardenfell; many residents mourn relatives on the island. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Year ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/topic-morrowind-in-the-4th-era
- 4E 6: sacked by the Argonians in the Accession War. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mournhold ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War
- Later: Royal Palace unoccupied; Helseth and family's fate unknown; still empty in the 3rd century 4E. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mournhold

### Blacklight (mainland, north-west, near Skyrim border)
- Controlling faction / ruler: House Redoran capital, governing the Redoran District of north-western Morrowind. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Blacklight
- 4E 5/6: becomes the permanent capital of Morrowind; the Grand Council meets in the Rootspire. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Blacklight ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Redoran
- Later: grows to rival Mournhold in size; has a Temple of Azura where Red Year survivor Neria Relethyl convalesces years later. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Blacklight ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/red-year-vol-i

### Tear (mainland, south; Dres)
- Controlling faction / ruler: House Dres. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Dres
- Economy: historic Dres plantation/slave-market city [unverified; should live at https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Tear].
- 4E 5: earthquakes; the city partially floods and sinks into the swampland it is built on. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Tear
- 4E 6: in the path of the Argonian advance across southern Morrowind; its fate is unknown. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Tear

### Necrom (mainland, east coast)
- Controlling faction / ruler: CONFLICT — listed among Dres-ruled settlements (House Dres page, per search summary) vs. rulership passing to House Indoril after it absorbed the Temple and the Dead Keepers in the 4E (Necrom page). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Dres ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Necrom
- Economy & customs: centre of ancestor worship in Morrowind (City of the Dead). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Necrom
- 4E 6: no source describes its role in the Accession War. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War

### Narsis (mainland, south-west; Hlaalu capital)
- Controlling faction / ruler: House Hlaalu capital. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Narsis
- 4E 5: refuge for Vivec City survivors on the south road (Saldus Llervu flees there). — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/red-year-vol-ii
- After Hlaalu's expulsion: "it is unknown what happened to their capital of Narsis". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Narsis

### Solstheim & Raven Rock
- 4E 4: Imperial/East Empire Company ebony-mining colony at Raven Rock [unverified]; Fort Frostmoth garrison, much of it recalled in 3E 433. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:History_of_Raven_Rock
- 4E 5: southern Solstheim devastated by ash; Fort Frostmoth destroyed. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Year
- 4E 5–16: Raven Rock replaces its garrison with elite Redoran Guard; Brara Morvayn designs the Bulwark wall, funded by the East Empire Company. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Brara_Morvayn
- 4E 16: gifted to Morrowind by Skyrim's High King; Redoran control; Imperials leave. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- Later: Lleril Morvayn is Councilor by 4E 201; the Red Year books are found at Morvayn Manor. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Lleril_Morvayn ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:The_Red_Year,_Vol._II

### Eastern Skyrim (refugee destination)
- Dunmer refugees flee into eastern Skyrim after the disasters; the Jarls later declare Refugees' Rest a monument to those who fled Morrowind after the Red Year. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era
- Windhelm's Grey Quarter as refugee district: not confirmed by these searches [unverified].

## 3. Factions & people (who, stance, fate)

### Great Houses
- **House Redoran** — Warrior House; rises to dominance after the Oblivion Crisis, Red Year and invasion; runs Red Year relief from Mournhold; halts the Argonian advance; moves the capital to Blacklight. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Redoran ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War
- **House Redoran** — The Hlaalu believe Redoran engineered the Council reorganisation and have held a grudge ever since. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Hlaalu
- **House Hlaalu** — Pro-Imperial merchant House, scapegoated after the Crisis; stripped of Great House status and its Grand Council seat after the Red Year. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Hlaalu
- **House Hlaalu** — Under Kingpin Orvas Dren (late 3E) the Camonna Tong controlled Hlaalu councilors and no major Hlaalu decision passed without it. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Camonna_Tong

## 5. Culture & daily life (food, drink, religion, naming conventions with examples, festivals, slang)
- **Name of the people** — "Dunmer" = "dark elves"; "dark" is read as dark-skinned, gloomy, or "ill-favoured by fate". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dunmer
- **Language** — Dunmeris prefix "Dun-" carries connotations of darkness and being cursed; "Mora" means forest/wood (Balmora, Sadrith Mora, Tel Mora). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dark_Elvish
- **Language** — "Tel" = tower (Telvanni towers: Tel Naga, Tel Mithryn) [unverified]; "Ald" = old [unverified].
- **Naming convention** — Given name + family name (often a House or clan line), e.g. Vedam Dren, Orvas Dren, Brara Morvayn, Lleril Morvayn, Neria Relethyl, Saldus Llervu, Deros Dran, Melis Ravel, Adril Arano, Geldis Sadri, Gilvas Barelo, Fedris Hler, Ildari Sarothril; single-name nobles of a Great House may be styled "House + name" (e.g. Hlaalu Helseth). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hlaalu_Helseth ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:The_Red_Year,_Vol._I ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Tel_Mithryn ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fedris_Hler

- **Slang / honourifics** — "n'wah" (foreigner, insult), "s'wit", "fetcher", "outlander", "sera", "serjo", "muthsera" [unverified] (should live at https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dark_Elvish).
- **Food** — Kwama eggs and saltrice porridge with scuttle are everyday staples. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dunmer_Cuisine ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Saltrice
- **Drink** — Sujamma (strong spirit), greef (cheap comberry brandy), mazte (saltrice). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Alcoholic_Beverages
- **Death customs** — The dead are interred "in the proper Velothi fashion"; Necrom is the centre of ancestor worship. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Almalexia ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Necrom
- **Faith shift** — Tribunes become "saints" (a traditional way of honouring the most revered ancestors); Azura, Boethiah and Mephala reclaim their identity as the Reclamations, and the old Temple term "Anticipations" falls out of use. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:The_Reclamations ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Good_Daedra

- **Pilgrimage** — Dunmer now travel into the ash wastes to consult Ashlander Wise Women. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ashlanders
- **Xenophobia** — Anti-outlander sentiment (Camonna Tong ideology) and anger at Imperial indifference colour 4E Dunmer attitudes. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Camonna_Tong ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Morrowind
