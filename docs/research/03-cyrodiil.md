# Cyrodiil, the Empire and the Stormcrown Interregnum (3E 433 – c. 4E 40)
_Researcher notes for Ashfall Ledger. Status tags: (no tag) = verified via cited source; [unverified]; [fan]; CONFLICT._

> Scope: Cyrodiil and the Imperial centre from the Oblivion Crisis to the early Mede Dynasty, with focus on 4E 1–22
> (Ocato's regency, the Stormcrown Interregnum, Titus Mede I). Campaign years 4E 4–6 fall **inside Ocato's
> regency**: there is no Emperor, the Elder Council and Potentate Ocato rule, and the Empire is visibly fraying.
> Mirror URLs (en.m., pt., fr., content1.m.) are normalised to `https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:…`. Sources in the
> `Oblivion:`/`Online:` namespaces are UESP game pages (describe game content, weaker than `Lore:`). Imperial
> Library "Lore Notes" pages are fan-compiled excerpts of the licensed Greg Keyes novels. The previous researcher's
> search budget ran out before Dark Brotherhood, Thieves Guild, Knights of the Nine and Imperial Cult/Talos queries
> could run; those topics rely on tags below.

## 1. Timeline (dated facts, oldest → newest; focus 3E 427 – 4E 40)
- **c. 3E 380+** — Andel Indarys, a House Hlaalu trader from Morrowind, moves to Cyrodiil; he later becomes Count of Cheydinhal (rumoured to owe his rise to his friend Helseth and to Barenziah). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Andel_Indarys
- **3E 399** — After Jagar Tharn's defeat and Uriel Septim VII's restoration, the Altmer Ocato is appointed Imperial Battlemage and Lord High Chancellor of the Elder Council. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ocato_of_Firsthold
- **c. 3E 432** — Alessia marries Count Marius Caro of Leyawiin. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Alessia_Caro
- **3E 433** — Uriel Septim VII and all his known heirs are assassinated by the Mythic Dawn, beginning the Oblivion Crisis. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Oblivion_Crisis
- **3E 433** — Ocato, as head of the Elder Council, becomes the Empire's de facto ruler and leads it through the Crisis. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ocato_of_Firsthold
- **3E 433** — Kvatch is ruined in the first moments of the Crisis (Daedric Siege Crawler wrecks Castle Kvatch; Great Hall interior destroyed). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Oblivion:Kvatch_Castle
- **3E 433** — Countess Llathasa Indarys of Cheydinhal dies in suspicious circumstances; rumours implicate the Count. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Andel_Indarys
- **3E 433** — In Black Marsh the Hist, having foreseen the Crisis, summon many Argonians home; the An-Xileel party takes credit for the defence. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:An-Xileel
- **3E 433 (end of the Third Era)** — Martin Septim shatters the Amulet of Kings in the Temple of the One, becomes the Avatar of Akatosh, banishes Mehrunes Dagon and turns to stone. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Martin_Septim
- **3E 433** — The Liminal Barrier is permanently sealed by Akatosh; the Dragonfires become obsolete and are never relit. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Liminal_Barrier ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dragonfires
- **3E 433** — The Septim Dynasty is extinct; Ocato and the Elder Council are again the rulers of the Empire. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ocato_of_Firsthold
- **End of the Third Era** — By agreement between the Mane and the Empire, the Trans-Niben is annexed back into Cyrodiil and County Leyawiin; the Khajiiti Renrijra Krin later seek to reclaim it. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Leyawiin
- **After 3E 433** — The Blades fortify themselves in Cloud Ruler Temple, awaiting a new Dragonborn. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Cloud_Ruler_Temple
- **4E 1** — With no heir agreed, the Elder Council invokes the Elder Council Charter; Ocato "reluctantly" becomes **Potentate** "until imperial rule was reestablished". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Elder_Council
- **4E 5** — The Red Year: Red Mountain erupts, devastating Vvardenfell and southern Solstheim. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Year ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- **4E 5 – c. 4E 6** — Accession War: Argonians invade Morrowind in the months after the Red Year; by mid-4E 6 they reach Telvanni lands and are halted by House Redoran's re-established army. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War
- **4E 5–10** — "The Empire begins to collapse"; Black Marsh secedes after the explosion of Red Mountain. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era (CONFLICT on cause/date, see §6)
- **4E 10** — Potentate Ocato assassinated, "likely" by Thalmor agents, starting the Stormcrown Interregnum (per UESP Fourth Era timeline, Alinor page, and Imperial Library timeline). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Alinor_(city) ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era — **CONFLICT** with c. 4E 15 (§6)
- **c. 4E 15** — Ocato assassinated (per his own UESP article and the Stormcrown Interregnum article; date inferred by counting seven years back from c. 4E 22). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ocato_of_Firsthold ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Stormcrown_Interregnum
- **4E 10/15 → 4E 17/22** — Stormcrown Interregnum: "seven-year-long period of infighting over the Ruby Throne"; the Elder Council fractures and many claim the throne "by force of arms or by some blood claim". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mede_Dynasty ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era
- **4E 16** — The High King of Skyrim cedes Solstheim to Morrowind for Dunmer refugees; Raven Rock becomes a Redoran holding and its Imperial residents leave for Cyrodiil. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock
- **4E 17** — "Titus Mede, a Colovian warlord, takes control of the Imperial City and the Empire"; Skyrim endorses his ascension (Imperial Library timeline citing *Rising Threat*). — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era — **CONFLICT** with c. 4E 22 (§6)
- **4E 18** — Attrebus Mede born (Imperial Library timeline only). — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era — **CONFLICT** with 4E 26 (§6)
- **4E 20** — The Jarls of Skyrim decree Refugees' Rest as a monument to Dunmer who fled Morrowind. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Morrowind
- **4E 22** — The Thalmor overthrow the Altmeri monarchy in a coup, take Summerset and rename it Alinor. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Summerset_Isles ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Thalmor ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Kingdom_of_Alinor
- **c. 4E 22** — Titus Mede captures the Imperial City with "less than a thousand men", deposes Thules the Gibbering, is crowned Emperor of Tamriel and founds the Mede Dynasty, ending the Interregnum. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Titus_Mede_I ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Stormcrown_Interregnum
- **c. 4E 22** — Hierem, minister to Thules until then, persuades the Elder Council to treat Titus as liberator, not conqueror. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hierem
- **4E 26** — Prince Attrebus Mede born to Titus I and his wife. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Attrebus_Mede ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Titus_Mede_I
- **4E 29** — Thalmor-backed coup in Valenwood; union with Alinor forms the third Aldmeri Dominion, which then severs contact with the Empire for ~70 years. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Valenwood ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Great_War ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era
- **4E 29 – 4E 48** — The Empire keeps supplying Bosmer loyalist rebels in Valenwood. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Valenwood
- **Before 4E 48** — The Mages Guild has been dissolved, succeeded by the Synod and the College of Whispers. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mages_Guild ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Synod
- **Before 4E 48** — An-Xileel wrest Lilmoth from the Empire. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Lilmoth
- **4E 48** — Umbriel Crisis; ends with a siege of the Imperial City; Hierem (by then prime minister) is killed; An-Xileel hold over Argonians appears to break. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Umbriel_Crisis ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hierem ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:An-Xileel
- **Later context (beyond scope)** — Void Nights 4E 98–100; Elsweyr coup 4E 115; Titus Mede II accedes 4E 168; Great War from 4E 171; Aldmeri push deeper into Cyrodiil 4E 172; Battle of the Red Ring and White-Gold Concordat 4E 175 (Blades disbanded, Talos worship outlawed). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Void_Nights ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Elsweyr ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial_City ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Blades

## 2. Places (status in 4E 4, 4E 5, 4E 6, and later if known)

### Imperial City (City Isle, Lake Rumare)
- Controlling faction / ruler: Elder Council under Potentate Ocato (4E 1 → 4E 10/15); then contested; Thules the Gibbering at some point in the Interregnum; Titus Mede I from 4E 17/22. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ocato_of_Firsthold ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Thules_the_Gibbering
- Economy & signature goods: the Market District is the city's designated trading district, "considered one of the best across the province". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial_City
- Economy: the Waterfront is the city's main port-of-call and home to poorer residents and much illicit activity. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial_City
- 4E 4–6: seat of Ocato's regency; no Emperor sits the Ruby Throne; the Elder Council governs from White-Gold Tower. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era
- Later: captured c. 4E 22 by Titus Mede with under a thousand men; besieged in the Umbriel Crisis 4E 48; held by the Dominion during the Great War until retaken at the Battle of the Red Ring 4E 175. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Titus_Mede_I ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Umbriel_Crisis ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Cyrodiil
- Atmosphere — **White-Gold Tower** (also Imperial Palace, White-Stone Tower, "White-Gold"): the enormous central spire. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:White-Gold_Tower
- Atmosphere — the **Elder Council Chamber** lies on the ground/lower floors of the palace; in 3E 433 a large table holds High Chancellor Ocato's chair and twenty-nine others. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Elder_Council ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Oblivion:Imperial_Palace
- Atmosphere — **Arboretum**: botanical gardens with statues of each of the Divines and Talos at the centre; houses the Imperial Academy of Records and Histories, a scholars' haunt. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial_City
- Atmosphere — **Talos Plaza** (formerly the Nobles District): wealthy residents; the Tiber Septim Hotel, most luxurious in the city. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial_City
- Atmosphere — **Elven Gardens**: residential; historically home of the Sintav clan (Second Era). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial_City
- Atmosphere — **Waterfront**: on an island SW of City Isle; reputed home of the "mythical" Thieves Guild of Cyrodiil. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial_City
- Atmosphere — **Temple District / Temple of the One**: heavily damaged in 3E 433; the stone Avatar of Akatosh stands in it, its wings extending beyond the walls. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Temple_of_the_One
- Atmosphere — a large statue of Akatosh, wings outstretched, stood at the centre of Talos Plaza in the late Third Era. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Statues/Aedra
- Atmosphere — the **Arcane University** (Mages Guild HQ since the Second Era) remained a centre of magical study even in 4E 201, after the Guild's dissolution. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Arcane_University
- Note: ESO-era (2E) names differ — "Memorial District" (Market District made a graveyard in the *Second Era* Interregnum), "Nobles District". Do not apply to 4E. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial_City
- Sources: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial_City ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:White-Gold_Tower ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:City_Isle

### Cheydinhal (County Cheydinhal, east, near the Morrowind border)
- Controlling faction / ruler: Count Andel Indarys, ex-Hlaalu Dunmer; in 3E 433 the only non-Imperial count in Cyrodiil; old Cyrodiilic families saw him as a "presumptuous upstart". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Andel_Indarys
- Economy & signature goods: no source on 4E economy; Dunmer migrants settled mainly here, shaping culture. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dunmer
- 4E 4–6: as the Cyrodiilic city with the strongest Dunmer ties, likely a refugee destination after the Red Year. — [unverified]
- Atmosphere: architecture with "heavy Dunmer influence" owing to proximity to Morrowind. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Cheydinhal
- Atmosphere: the Indarys thorn motif (Staff of Indarys, Thornblade) is the county's symbol, borne by the Knights of the Thorn. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Indarys ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Staff_of_Indarys
- Atmosphere: the Count "knows what's inside the Abandoned House" — the local Dark Brotherhood sanctuary — but won't speak of it. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Oblivion:Count_Andel_Indarys
- Sources: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Cheydinhal ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Andel_Indarys

### Leyawiin (County Leyawiin, Trans-Niben, Topal Bay)
- Controlling faction / ruler: House Caro (ruling since the early Second Empire); Count Marius Caro rules in the late Third and early Fourth Eras with Countess Alessia. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Marius_Caro
- Economy & signature goods: estuary port at the southern end of the Green Road on Topal Bay; considerable Khajiit and Argonian population. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Leyawiin ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Green_Road
- 4E 4–6: frontier city facing a Black Marsh that is seceding under the An-Xileel (4E 5–10). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era
- Atmosphere: straddles a swampy estuary of the Niben on the strip between Elsweyr and Black Marsh. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Leyawiin
- Atmosphere: the Caros' "Imperialization" policy divides citizens; Alessia is hostile to Argonians, Khajiit and Dunmer. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Alessia_Caro
- Atmosphere: rumours of a secret torture chamber in Castle Leyawiin for Argonian prisoners. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Castle_Leyawiin (presented as rumour on other pages, see §6)
- Sources: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Leyawiin ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Marius_Caro ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Alessia_Caro

- Controlling faction / ruler: Countess Narina Carvain in 3E 433. — [unverified] (Lore:Bruma)

### Chorrol (Great Forest / Colovian Highlands)
- Location: between the Great Forest and the Colovian Highlands, north-west Cyrodiil. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Cyrodiil
- Ruler/economy: Countess Arriana Valga in 3E 433; the Great Oak; Fighters Guild hall. — [unverified] (Lore:Chorrol)

### Colovia (western Cyrodiil)
- Homeland of Titus Mede I, a Colovian warlord. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Colovia
- Before 4E 22 Titus had been "a soldier in an outlaw army, a warlord in Colovia, and a king in Cyrodiil". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Titus_Mede_I
- The Elder Council's preference for the Nibenese Thules over a "Colovian usurper" shows the Colovia–Nibenay rift during the Interregnum. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Thules_the_Gibbering
- West Weald is "well known for its vineyards". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Cyrodiil

### Roads & river travel
- **Red Ring Road** circles Lake Rumare and the Imperial City and links to most major roads; crosses an "impressively large bridge" over the Upper Niben. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Ring_Road
- **Green Road** runs south from the Red Ring Road through the Great Forest along the western Niben, via Bravil, to Leyawiin on Topal Bay. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Green_Road
- Some maps show the Green Road continuing south into Elsweyr (absent in *Oblivion*). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Green_Road
- **Gold Road** runs east–west from the Imperial City to Anvil via Skingrad and Kvatch. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Oblivion:Gold_Road
- **Niben River / Niben Bay**: Niben Bay is the large lake carrying Lake Rumare's outflow to Topal Bay; the Heartlands' southern edge runs down the Niben. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Heartlands
- Other roads: Silver Road (IC–Bruma), Black Road (IC–Chorrol), Blue Road (IC–Cheydinhal), Yellow Road (Bravil–Leyawiin side). — [unverified] (Lore:Roads / Lore:Places pages)

## 3. Factions & people (who, stance, fate)
- **Ocato of Firsthold** — Altmer battlemage; Imperial Battlemage and High Chancellor from 3E 399; de facto ruler after 3E 433; Potentate from 4E 1. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ocato_of_Firsthold
- Ocato reportedly took the Thalmor's re-emergence more seriously than most, possibly because he was Altmer. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ocato_of_Firsthold
- Ocato served as Potentate "reluctantly" and "stewarded the Empire for over a decade" before his assassination. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mede_Dynasty
- Name note: "Ocato of Firsthold" comes from the Imperial Library / possibly the Daggerfall manual; in-game he is "High Chancellor Ocato". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore_talk:People_O
- During the Crisis Ocato said the Legion generals told him provincial forces were "fully committed" and that recalling troops would cause a political crisis. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Oblivion_Crisis
- **Elder Council** — normally convenes in the Elder Council Chamber of the Imperial Palace; its Charter lets it appoint the High Chancellor as Potentate if no heir can be chosen. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Elder_Council
- After Ocato's death the Council fractured; it backed the Nibenese Thules over a Colovian usurper. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Thules_the_Gibbering
- **Thules the Gibbering** — Nibenese witch-warrior (also called battlemage) who assumed control of the weakened Empire during the Interregnum; unpopular; deposed c. 4E 22. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Thules_the_Gibbering ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Colovia
- **Other Interregnum claimants** — unnamed; *Rising Threat IV* says "a few" had "legitimate claims" but none ruled long. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dragonborn ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Rising_Threat,_Vol._IV
- "Legitimate" claims elsewhere in lore usually mean descent from the royal family; a UESP user essay doubts any claimant was Dragonborn. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/User:Mindtrait0r/AmbiguityOfDragonblood (user page, not lore)
- **Titus Mede I** — born in Colovia, birth date unknown; outlaw soldier → Colovian warlord → "king in Cyrodiil" → Emperor (c. 4E 22); founder of the Mede Dynasty. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Titus_Mede_I
- Titus "proved a shrewd and capable leader, such that Skyrim endorsed him as Emperor" (*Rising Threat IV*). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Rising_Threat,_Vol._IV
- Titus had unidentified brothers; son Attrebus; Attrebus's succession is assumed, never stated. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Titus_Mede_I ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mede_Dynasty
- **Hierem** — old Nibenese family; Mages Guild leadership before its dissolution; later powerful in the Synod; minister to Thules until c. 4E 22. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hierem
- Hierem's support "assisted greatly in smoothing over the conquest"; by 4E 48 he was Titus I's prime minister and the second most powerful man in the Empire; Titus distrusted him but feared civil war via his Synod influence; killed in the Umbriel Crisis. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hierem ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Stormcrown_Interregnum
- **Attrebus Mede** — crown prince by 4E 48; one of the heroes who banished Umbriel. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Attrebus_Mede
- **Lathenil of Sunhold** — Altmer exile, author of *Rising Threat*; describes Thalmor consolidation of Summerset and blames them for Ocato's death; the series ends with notice of his murder. Bias: anti-Thalmor refugee. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Rising_Threat,_Vol._IV
- **Thalmor** — "believed" to have ordered Ocato's assassination; seized Summerset 4E 22 and Valenwood 4E 29. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ocato_of_Firsthold ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Thalmor
- Thalmor agents had formed close ties to certain Bosmer factions even before the Oblivion Crisis. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:The_Great_War_(book)
- **Mages Guild** — dissolved early in the Fourth Era (before 4E 48); decline attributed to anti-magic sentiment after the Oblivion Crisis. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mages_Guild ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hannibal_Traven

## 4. Goods, trade & laws relevant to this area
- **Currency**: gold coins called septims or drakes (sign "dr"), named after Tiber Septim's conquests; coins bear Tiber Septim's face and the Red Dragon. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Currency
- Septims stay in use through the Fourth Era (still the coin of Skyrim, 4E 201); no Mede-specific coinage recorded. — [unverified] (Lore:Currency)
- **Cyrodiil exports**: textiles, fabrics, moon sugar, rice and armour. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial
- **Skingrad**: wine, tomatoes, cheese; West Weald vintages (Surilie Brothers, Tamika). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Skingrad
- **Gold Coast**: farms; Anvil harbour trade. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Cyrodiil ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Oblivion:Anvil
- **Bravil**: river-port trade on Niben Bay. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Bravil
- **Imperial City Market District**: the province's premier trading district. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial_City
- **East Empire Company (EEC)**: Imperial merchant body chartered by the Emperor and run by an Emperor-appointed board of directors. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:East_Empire_Company
- EEC exclusive rights (Morrowind/Solstheim): raw ebony, raw glass, Stalhrim, Dwemer artefacts; favourable tariffs on Vvardenfell imports/exports. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:East_Empire_Company
- EEC had "unclear dealings with the Thalmor" in the Fourth Era; full name "East Empire Trading Company" unverified; link to the (Second Era) Imperial Trade Service unknown. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:East_Empire_Company
- With no Emperor in 4E 1–22, who holds the EEC charter is not recorded. — [unverified]
- **Contraband**: moon sugar is "illegal in Third Era Morrowind and the rest of the Empire"; skooma is its refined, more addictive form. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Moon_Sugar
- Note the tension: Cyrodiil is listed as an *exporter* of moon sugar, yet it is illegal Empire-wide. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Moon_Sugar
- In *Oblivion* Cyrodiilic merchants buy and sell skooma freely (game mechanic, weak evidence of lax enforcement). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Oblivion:Skooma
- Imperial Regulators searched visitors for moon sugar outside Rimmen in Elsweyr (Second Era precedent for customs checks). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial_Legion
- **Talos worship legal** throughout this period; outlawed only by the White-Gold Concordat in 4E 175. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Blades
- **Necromancy**: banned by the Synod (later 4E); the College of Whispers tolerates it. Legal status in 4E 4–6 (pre-dissolution Mages Guild) is not recorded; the Mages Guild banned necromancy under Traven in 3E 433. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Synod ; [unverified] for the Traven ban (Lore:Mages_Guild)
- **Refugee flows**: Raven Rock's Imperials leave for Cyrodiil after 4E 16; Dunmer refugees settle in Skyrim (Windhelm) and Solstheim. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Morrowind
- Bounty/jail rules found are Morrowind game mechanics, not Cyrodiil law; not used. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Morrowind:Crime

## 5. Culture & daily life
- **Names**: Cyrodiilic names echo ancient Rome and Romance-speaking lands (design note). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial
- Examples from sources: Titus Mede, Attrebus Mede, Marius Caro, Alessia Caro, Savlian Matius, Tertius Ione, Justianus Quintius, Hannibal Traven, Uriel/Martin Septim, Varen Aquilarios, Hierem, Thules. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Titus_Mede_I ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Marius_Caro ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:The_Great_War_(book)
- Colovian names may lean Slavic/Eastern European (Skingrad, Rislav) — forum speculation only. — [unverified]
- Some Nibenese names (Zurin Arctus, Jagar Tharn) may be corrupted Akaviri or Ayleid originals — fan theory. — [unverified]
- **Looks**: Imperials are often shown with medium-fair to olive/tan skin and dark hair. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial
- **Colovia vs. Nibenay**: Colovian martial tradition (organisation, equipment) and Nibenese architecture are Roman-inspired. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial
- Colovians call Shezarr ("Shezzar") "Shor", from Nordic influence. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Colovia
- Nibenese favour tattoos, ornament, mysticism and commerce; Colovians are austere, martial highlanders. — [unverified] (Lore:Imperial)
- **Religion**: the Nine Divines incl. Talos; Arboretum statues of each Divine with Talos at the centre. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial_City
- "Talos" means "Stormcrown" in the Ehlnofey tongue; Nords so named Hjalti after the storm-aided assault on Old Hrol'dan. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Tiber_Septim ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Wulfharth
- No source explains why the 4E interregnum is called "Stormcrown". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Stormcrown_Interregnum (no etymology given)
- Around the Fourth Era, temple statuettes of Akatosh show him swallowing a sword; meaning unclear. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Akatosh
- The stone Avatar of Akatosh in the Temple District is a pilgrimage focus (in-game it grants the "Favor of Akatosh" blessing). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Oblivion:The_Temple_of_the_One
- "Conjecture about what actually happened is rampant across Tamriel" regarding Martin's battle — good tavern talk. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Martin_Septim
- **Food & drink**: Skingrad wine, tomatoes, cheese; Cyrodiilic rice. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Skingrad ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial
- Cyrodilic brandy, Surilie Brothers vintages, Tamika's vintages as named drinks. — [unverified] (Lore:Alcoholic_Beverages)
- **Festivals** (Tamriel-wide calendar): New Life Festival 1 Morning Star (campaign start day), South Wind's Prayer 15 Morning Star, Heart's Day 16 Sun's Dawn, Mid Year Celebration 16 Mid Year, Emperor's Day 30 Frostfall (awkward with no Emperor in 4E 1–22), Saturalia 25 Evening Star. — [unverified] (Lore:Holidays)
- **Social friction**: old Cyrodiilic families resented a Dunmer count; the Caros pushed Imperialization of Leyawiin's mixed population. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Andel_Indarys ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Marius_Caro
- **Anti-magic mood** after the Oblivion Crisis eroded the Mages Guild. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hannibal_Traven
- **Dunmer view**: the Empire's "ineffectual protection and seeming indifference" after the Red Year made it reviled by the Dunmer — expect cold treatment of Imperial traders in Morrowind. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Morrowind

## 6. Conflicts & uncertainties
- **CONFLICT: Ocato's death — 4E 10 vs c. 4E 15.** 4E 10: UESP Fourth Era timeline, UESP Alinor (city), Imperial Library timeline (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Alinor_(city) ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era). c. 4E 15: UESP Ocato and Stormcrown Interregnum articles, which say no precise date is ever given and back-calculate 7 years from c. 4E 22 (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ocato_of_Firsthold ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Stormcrown_Interregnum). Default: c. 4E 15 (current UESP article). Either way, Ocato is alive for the whole 4E 4–6 campaign.
- **CONFLICT: Titus Mede I takes the Imperial City — 4E 17 vs c. 4E 22.** 4E 17: Imperial Library timeline citing *Rising Threat* (https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era). c. 4E 22: UESP Titus Mede I, Mede Dynasty, Stormcrown Interregnum, Cyrodiil, Fourth Era (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Titus_Mede_I ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mede_Dynasty). Both keep "seven years" (4E 10→17 / 4E 15→22). Default: c. 4E 22.
- **CONFLICT (likely error): 4E 168.** One UESP "Lore:Empires" entry gives 4E 168 for Mede's seizure, apparently confusing Titus Mede II's accession (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Empires).
- **CONFLICT: Attrebus Mede's birth — 4E 18 vs 4E 26.** 4E 18: Imperial Library timeline (https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era). 4E 26: UESP Attrebus Mede, Titus Mede I, Fourth Era (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Attrebus_Mede). Default: 4E 26.
- **CONFLICT: Who killed Ocato.** Thalmor per *Rising Threat IV* (anti-Thalmor Altmer exile) and UESP ("it was believed", "likely") (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Rising_Threat,_Vol._IV ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ocato_of_Firsthold). A UESP talk page says the claim rests only on *Rising Threat* and should be softened (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore_talk:Fourth_Era). Present as rumour.
- **CONFLICT: Size of Titus's army.** "Less than a thousand men" (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Titus_Mede_I) vs "only one thousand men" (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Colovia).
- **CONFLICT: Black Marsh secession — when and why.** (a) 4E 5–10, after the Red Mountain eruption (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era); (b) seceded together with Elsweyr after the Oblivion Crisis and the eruption (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Black_Marsh); (c) after the Thalmor incited an Argonian uprising against southern Morrowind (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Kingdom_of_Black_Marsh); (d) "lost to Imperial rule since the Oblivion Crisis" (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era).
- **CONFLICT: Were the Legions recalled from the provinces during the Crisis?** Yes — Redoran Councilor Adril Arano, ~200 years later; Redoran Guard formed "after the withdrawal" (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ald'ruhn ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Factions_R). No — Ocato says provincial forces were fully committed; no foreign legions were seen in Cyrodiil during the Crisis (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Oblivion_Crisis). A talk page notes Ocato could have recalled troops later (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore_talk:Oblivion_Crisis).
- **CONFLICT: When the Mages Guild dissolved.** "At the start of the Fourth Era" (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Synod) vs "some point before 4E 48" (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mages_Guild) vs "presumably dissolved after [the Oblivion] crisis" (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Travelers_League). Whether the Guild still exists in 4E 4–6 is therefore open.
- **CONFLICT: Elsweyr's secession.** Seceded (with Black Marsh) around the Void Nights (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:History) vs remained Imperial until the 4E 115 coup per *The Great War* (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore_talk:Fourth_Era ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Elsweyr).
- **CONFLICT: Morrowind's status after 4E 5.** "Effectively independent", though some Imperials still call it a province (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War); the Empire "released its grasp" may mean loosening control, not full independence (https://www.imperial-library.info/content/topic-morrowind-in-the-4th-era).
- **CONFLICT: Umbriel's route (4E 48).** North out of the south into Cyrodiil (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Umbriel_Crisis) vs appearing on the Black Marsh coast and heading toward Morrowind (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era).
- **CONFLICT: Imperial City statues/layout.** Akatosh statue at the centre of Talos Plaza (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Statues/Aedra) vs Talos statue at the centre of the Arboretum (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Imperial_City); district names/counts differ between *Oblivion* and ESO pages.
- **CONFLICT: Countess Alessia Caro.** "Uncomfortable" with Argonian/Khajiit/Dunmer ways (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Alessia_Caro) vs "despises" them (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Oblivion:Countess_Alessia_Caro); torture chamber is rumour on most pages but stated as fact on https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Castle_Leyawiin.
- **CONFLICT: Valenwood 4E 29 coup.** Overthrow of an Imperial-friendly government (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Valenwood) vs a brief campaign capturing Imperial strongholds (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Titus_Mede_I).
- **CONFLICT: Era boundary.** The Amulet's destruction is "at the turning of the Fourth Age, in the year 3E 433" (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:The_Oblivion_Crisis); the year-0/4E 1 question is debated (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore_talk:Fourth_Era).
- **Uncertainty: Varen Aquilarios is a *Second Era* Interregnum figure**, not a Stormcrown claimant, despite one search summary implying otherwise (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dragonborn ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Second_Era_Wars). The 2E "Interregnum" (2E 430–854) is a different period.
- **Uncertainty: Kvatch's rebuilding** is promised but never shown; no 4E source (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Oblivion:Kvatch_Castle).
- **Uncertainty: Interregnum claimants other than Thules and Titus are unnamed** in all sources found (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Stormcrown_Interregnum).
- **Uncertainty: the Solstheim grant (4E 16)** is not tied to Titus Mede in any source (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim).
- **Uncertainty: no causal link** between the Thalmor coup in Summerset (4E 22) and Titus Mede's capture of the Imperial City (c. 4E 22) is recorded (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Summerset_Isles).
