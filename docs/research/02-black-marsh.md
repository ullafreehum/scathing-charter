# Black Marsh, the An-Xileel and the Argonian Invasion of Morrowind (Accession War), 3E 427 – c. 4E 48
_Researcher notes for Ashfall Ledger. Status tags: (no tag) = verified via cited source; [unverified]; [fan]; CONFLICT._

> Scope note: these notes cover Black Marsh from Imperial rule to the Umbriel disaster (4E 48), with most detail on
> 4E 4–6: the Hist and the Oblivion Crisis, the An-Xileel, secession, and the invasion of Morrowind.
> The invasion is thinly sourced in canon. Most of what exists comes from UESP synthesis pages, Skyrim and Dragonborn dialogue,
> the Greg Keyes novels (The Infernal City, Lord of Souls) and the in-game book *Rising Threat, Vol. IV*. Expect many CONFLICT bullets.
> Source-bias key: *Rising Threat* = anti-Thalmor Altmer polemicist; *Pocket Guide to the Empire* and *The Argonian Account* = Imperial viewpoints;
> Adril Arano = Redoran official; the An-Xileel accounts of the Oblivion Crisis are self-promotion.
> Search budget note: the earlier researcher's last three name searches ("Heem-Jas", "Teeba-Enai", "Wanders-the-Marsh") were never run because the search limit was reached. Name examples that were not confirmed are tagged.

## 1. Timeline (dated facts, oldest → newest; focus 3E 427 – 4E 40)
- **1E 2837** — Reman II controls the northern and eastern parts of Black Marsh and declares them the Imperial Province of Black Marsh. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Black_Marsh
- **1E 2838 (?)** — Another account says Black Marsh was "officially incorporated" into the Cyrodilic Empire "the following year" (see §6). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Black_Marsh
- **Early Imperial period** — The Empire used Black Marsh as a dumping ground for prisoners "too dangerous to hold in 'civilized' dungeons" in other provinces. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Black_Marsh
- **2E (Three Banners War era)** — The Ebonheart Pact's Dunmer houses free their Argonian slaves and nominally outlaw slavery. House Telvanni stays outside the Pact and keeps slaves. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Slavery
- **2E (Ebonheart Pact)** — Stormhold is named capital of Black Marsh, at least for the Pact-affiliated parts. In the mid-Second Era, Stormhold and Thorn are under Dunmer rule. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Stormhold
- **Third Era (Armistice)** — Slavery is illegal throughout the Empire, but the Armistice protects it by law in Morrowind. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Slavery
- **Third Era** — Dunmer slave traders are "freer than ever" to exploit Black Marsh, and "entire tribes of Argonians were dragged in chains" to Morrowind. House Dres keeps sending slavers south. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Black_Marsh ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Pocket_Guide_to_the_Empire,_3rd_Edition/Argonia
- **3E 427** — Slavery is still legal in Morrowind, and Argonian and Khajiit slaves are a common sight on Vvardenfell (TES III). [unverified] — should be at https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Morrowind:Slaves
- **3E 430** — King Helseth of Morrowind decrees the abolition of slavery. One source says the decree sparked a civil war. Another says he issued it six months into the fighting, as a strategic move to incite slave revolts in Indoril and Redoran lands (see §6). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Slavery
- **Late 3E** — An Imperial guide says that "since the abolishment of slavery, Black Marsh's relationship with its northern neighbour Morrowind has been much improved." — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Pocket_Guide_to_the_Empire,_3rd_Edition/Argonia
- **3E 433 (Oblivion Crisis)** — The Hist foresee Mehrunes Dagon's invasion and call Argonians abroad home to Black Marsh before the Daedra arrive. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Argonian ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Oblivion_Crisis
- **3E 433** — The year of the Oblivion Crisis itself is not stated in the search results. [unverified] — https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Oblivion_Crisis
- **3E 433** — The Hist alter the Argonians to fight the Daedra, making them "faster, stronger, and able to endure harsher punishment". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Argonian
- **3E 433** — Argonians charge into the Oblivion Gates in Black Marsh so fiercely that the Daedra themselves close the gates to avoid being overrun. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Argonian ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/infernal-city-lore-notes-black-marsh
- **3E 433** — The An-Xileel, a political party formed during the Crisis, take credit for organising the defence of Black Marsh. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:An-Xileel
- **After the Oblivion Crisis (c. 3E 433 – 4E early years)** — The An-Xileel unite most Argonians under their rule, turn on the Empire, declare Black Marsh independent and sack the Imperial colonies in the province. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Kingdom_of_Black_Marsh ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:An-Xileel
- **Post-Crisis** — Under the An-Xileel, Black Marsh is renamed **Argonia**. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:An-Xileel ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Black_Marsh
- **4E 1 – 4E 29 (window)** — One UESP page dates Black Marsh's break with the Empire to this window, together with Elsweyr and Summerset. Another dates it to 4E 5–10 (see §6). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era
- **4E 5 (the Red Year)** — Baar Dau falls on Vivec City and Red Mountain erupts, wrecking Vvardenfell. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Year ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Vvardenfell
- **4E 5** — No exact day or month for the eruption turned up in the results. [unverified] — https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Year
- **4E 5** — Brand-Shei (born Brandyl Tenvanni, of House Telvanni blood) is born. He is later found wrapped in a Telvanni blanket and raised by Argonians in Black Marsh. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:People_B ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Brand-Shei
- **4E 5, "in the months following the Red Year"** — The Argonians, led by the An-Xileel, invade crippled Morrowind. The Imperial Library says they were "urged on by the Hist". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era
- **4E 5–6** — The Imperial Library timeline dates the invasion to 4E 5–6. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era
- **4E 6** — The UESP Thalmor article says the Thalmor incited the Argonians to launch the Accession War against Morrowind and the Empire in 4E 6. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Thalmor ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Rising_Threat,_Vol._IV
- **4E 5–6** — Mournhold is sacked during the war, and much of southern and eastern Morrowind is devastated. This is contested (see §6). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mournhold
- **By mid-4E 6** — The Argonians have pushed deep into House Telvanni territory in northeastern Morrowind. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War
- **c. 4E 6** — The House Telvanni page says the last remaining Telvanni stronghold was sacked and the Great House fell. A talk page disputes this (see §6). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Telvanni ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore_talk:House_Telvanni
- **4E 6 (approx.)** — A Telvanni matron flees Morrowind "during the Accession War" aboard the ship *Pride of Tel Vos*, according to Brand-Shei's research. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Brand-Shei
- **4E 6** — The "newly re-established" army of House Redoran prevents the complete occupation of Morrowind and halts the invasion. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Redoran
- **4E 5–10** — Black Marsh secedes from the Empire "after the explosion of Red Mountain", alongside Elsweyr, per one UESP timeline. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era
- **Between 4E 6 and 4E 28** — The Argonians stop advancing. UESP gives no firm end date. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War
- **c. 4E 28** — UESP's Wars list gives the war as 4E 6 – c. 4E 28. The talk page treats c. 4E 28 as an estimate, not a canonical event. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Wars ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore_talk:Accession_War
- **Post-war** — House Redoran moves Morrowind's capital to Blacklight and becomes the dominant House. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Blacklight ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Great_Houses
- **Unspecified 4E** — The Thalmor lose their influence over the Argonians, and Black Marsh is "lost to the An-Xileel". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Thalmor
- **4E 40** — The opening framing of *The Infernal City* is dated 4E 40 ("Tamriel is threatened anew…"). UESP records no Umbriel event in that year. — Source: https://forums.uesp.net/viewtopic.php?f=30&t=15454
- **c. 4E 43** — The Imperial Library timeline dates Umbriel's arrival and the destruction of Lilmoth to this year. This conflicts with 4E 48 (see §6). — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era
- **4E 47** — The Hierem page says Prime Minister Hierem and the An-Xileel performed the ritual at Lilmoth's Hist tree that summoned Umbriel. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hierem
- **Before 4E 48** — The An-Xileel have wrested Lilmoth from the Empire and bar all unlicensed foreigners from the city. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Kingdom_of_Black_Marsh ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Lilmoth
- **By 4E 48** — Argonians occupy the area around Scathing Bay, where Vivec City once stood. Some sources say they hold southern Vvardenfell. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Vivec_City ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/topic-morrowind-in-the-4th-era
- **4E 48** — In *Lord of Souls*, an Imperial character says the An-Xileel "stopped their advance [in Morrowind] decades ago, and haven't showed the slightest interest in doing anything since then." — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/lord-of-souls-lore-notes-black-marsh
- **4E 48** — Umbriel appears off the coast of Black Marsh south of Lilmoth, summoned by the rogue Hist of Lilmoth working with the An-Xileel. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Umbriel ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:An-Xileel
- **4E 48** — The "Slaughter at Lilmoth": Umbriel draws out the souls of Lilmoth's people and the city is destroyed. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Lilmoth ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era
- **4E 48** — Umbriel's undead army overruns Stormhold and Gideon. Lilmoth, Gideon and Stormhold are "stripped of life". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Stormhold ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era_Wars
- **4E 48** — Umbriel moves north through Black Marsh and southern Morrowind, by way of Scathing Bay, towards the Imperial City. Heroes later banish it by destroying the ingenium and killing Umbra. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Umbriel ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era_Wars
- **After 4E 48** — The disaster "appears to have broken the hold the An-Xileel held over the Argonian people". Their later status is unknown. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:An-Xileel
- **By Titus Mede I's reign** — Black Marsh and the Summerset Isles have both seceded. His accession is c. 4E 98. [unverified] — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Titus_Mede_I
- **4E 150** — A small Argonian force lands on Solstheim "bent on destruction" and is driven off by the Redoran Guard under Lleril Morvayn. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/topic-morrowind-in-the-4th-era
- **By 4E 201** — Mournhold is back under Dunmer control. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mournhold
- **4E 201** — Brand-Shei learns his true identity with the Last Dragonborn's help. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:People_B

## 2. Places (status in 4E 4, 4E 5, 4E 6, and later if known)

### Black Marsh / Argonia (province as a whole)
- Controlling faction / ruler: In the Third Era, Imperials ruled the coastal cities in the Empire's name, mostly with Argonian advisors called **Archeins**. Archeins also governed the rural areas that make up most of the province. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Pocket_Guide_to_the_Empire,_3rd_Edition/Argonia
- Controlling faction / ruler: The inner swamps had little supervision, and "it is unknown whether or not these areas even recognize Imperial rule". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Pocket_Guide_to_the_Empire,_3rd_Edition/Argonia
- Controlling faction / ruler (4E 4–6): The An-Xileel, after their post-Crisis secession. Their governing body is **"the Organism"**, based in Lilmoth. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:An-Xileel ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/infernal-city-lore-notes-black-marsh
- The state is called the **Kingdom of Black Marsh** or **Kingdom of Argonia**. UESP admits "almost nothing is known" about it. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Kingdom_of_Black_Marsh
- Economy & signature goods: The economy was mostly subsistence farming. Imperial schemes to move it to export crops are described as having failed to help. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Black_Marsh
- Economy & signature goods: **Washtongue powder**, a native mineral used in firing pottery, is the only specific trade good the search found. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Black_Marsh
- Economy & signature goods: Ancient Argonians chewed **dragonthorn** leaves to help produce healthy eggs. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Black_Marsh
- What happens 4E 4–6: An independent, An-Xileel-led Argonia has already purged colonial influence. In 4E 5 it invades Morrowind. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War
- What happens later: Umbriel devastates it in 4E 48 and the An-Xileel's hold breaks. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:An-Xileel
- Atmosphere: Hist trees stand at the heart of every settlement. Argonians lick sap from Hist trunks in religious rites. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hist
- Atmosphere: An Imperial clerk describes trying to run a road through the swamp, where goods spend months in transit and rot (see Imperial Commerce Road). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:The_Argonian_Account
- Atmosphere: Argonian step-pyramids (**xanmeer**) and stilt villages. [unverified] — https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Xanmeer
- Sources: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Black_Marsh ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Kingdom_of_Black_Marsh ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:An-Xileel

### Lilmoth
- Controlling faction / ruler: Historically there was no single ruler. A council of wealthy, influential merchants ran the town and dispensed justice to non-natives through an informal court. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Lilmoth ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/history-lilmoth
- Controlling faction / ruler (4E): The An-Xileel. The Organism sits here, and the party wrested the city from the Empire before 4E 48. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Lilmoth ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:An-Xileel
- Economy & signature goods: A merchant city and port on **Oliis Bay**, and historically the most centralised settlement in Murkmire. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Lilmoth
- Economy & trade law (4E): The An-Xileel "forbade all but licensed foreigners" in the city, so all foreign trade crowded into one market district. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/infernal-city-lore-notes-black-marsh
- Security: The **Thtachalxan** ("drykillers") are the only non-Argonian guards in Lilmoth. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/infernal-city-lore-notes-black-marsh
- Hist: Only the An-Xileel and "wild Argonians" have full access to the Lilmoth city-tree. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/lord-of-souls-lore-notes-black-marsh
- Hist: A Lilmoth tree went "rogue" over 300 years before *Lord of Souls* and was purged by the other Hist. The current Lilmoth Hist grew from part of its root. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/lord-of-souls-lore-notes-black-marsh
- History: The Knahaten Flu killed off the original **Lilmothiit** inhabitants. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/infernal-city-lore-notes-black-marsh
- What happens 4E 4–6: Under An-Xileel rule the city is the seat of the Organism. No specific 4E 5–6 events found. [unverified] — https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Lilmoth
- Later: The 4E 48 "Slaughter at Lilmoth": Umbriel draws out its people's souls and destroys the city. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Lilmoth
- Note: UESP's Lilmoth page contradicts itself on when the city's Argonian Priest-King line ended. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Lilmoth
- Sources: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Lilmoth ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/infernal-city-lore-notes-black-marsh ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/lord-of-souls-lore-notes-black-marsh

### Imperial Commerce Road (Gideon inland route)
- An Imperial commission spent decades building the road from Gideon. Goods still took two and a half months to arrive, "now thoroughly rotten". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:The_Argonian_Account
- The story follows Decumus Scotti, an Imperial clerk sent to take over the commission's Black Marsh account. The account is satirical and written from an Imperial viewpoint. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:The_Argonian_Account
- Status 4E 4–6: Probably abandoned or dismantled along with the other Imperial colonial assets. [unverified]

### Interior swamps / Murkmire

## 3. Factions & people (who, stance, fate)
- **An-Xileel** — A political party formed during the Oblivion Crisis, made up mainly if not entirely of Argonians. It pushed for independence and afterwards united most Argonians under its rule. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:An-Xileel
- **An-Xileel stance** — "Entirely nativistic", interested only in purging former colonial influences. This is an Imperial-perspective description. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/infernal-city-lore-notes-black-marsh
- **An-Xileel and the Hist** — Some sources call the An-Xileel the Hist's political arm. Others have the Hist (or a rogue tree) calling the shots. Later accounts say the rogue Lilmoth tree had controlled the party. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:An-Xileel
- **An-Xileel fate** — Their hold breaks after Umbriel (4E 48), and their later status is unknown. A UESP talk page questions whether they existed at all after *Lord of Souls*. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:An-Xileel ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore_talk:Tamriel
- **The Organism** — The An-Xileel governing body, seated in Lilmoth. A timeline entry says Black Marsh is "currently led by the An-Xileel and the Organism". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:An-Xileel ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/infernal-city-lore-notes-black-marsh

## 4. Goods, trade & laws relevant to this area (what is produced, what is contraband, tariffs, currency)
- **Project rule (not lore):** slaves and people are never a tradeable good in Ashfall Ledger. Slavery appears only as historical context, rumors and story consequences.
- **Washtongue powder** — A native mineral used in pottery firing, and the only Black Marsh trade good named in the results. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Black_Marsh
- **Herbs / hunting goods** — Gideon is visited by herb dealers and hunters. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Gideon
- **Hist sap** — Licked in religious rites and used to incubate eggs. Hardened sap is given to hatchlings as teething pacifiers. It is sacred and dangerous in large amounts. Nothing in canon describes it as a trade good. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hist ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Argonian
- **Dragonthorn leaves** — Chewed by ancient Argonians to help produce healthy eggs. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Black_Marsh
- **Amber plasm** — A UESP lore page exists for this Hist-related substance. Its contents were not retrieved. [unverified] — https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Amberplasm
- **Agriculture** — Mostly subsistence. Imperial export-crop schemes failed. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Black_Marsh
- **Imperial Commerce Road** — Notoriously slow (2½ months from Gideon), and goods arrived rotten. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:The_Argonian_Account
- **Foreigner licensing (Lilmoth, 4E)** — Only licensed foreigners may enter the city. Foreign trade is confined to one crowded market district. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/infernal-city-lore-notes-black-marsh
- **Justice for foreigners (Lilmoth, older)** — Merchant council courts judge non-natives informally. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/history-lilmoth
- **Imperial colonies** — Sacked by the An-Xileel after the Oblivion Crisis. Imperial trading posts and assets in Black Marsh are gone before 4E 4. Exact year unknown. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Kingdom_of_Black_Marsh
- **Currency** — The currency of independent Argonia (if any) was not found. Septims are presumably still used by licensed foreign traders. [unverified]
- **Sea routes** — Lilmoth (Oliis Bay), Archon (east coast) and Stormhold (river port) are the main maritime and river gateways. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Lilmoth ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Archon ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Stormhold
- **Slave trade (historical)** — Dunmer slavers, especially House Dres, took "entire tribes" north. Archeins collaborated. The trade was legal in Morrowind under the Armistice until Helseth's abolition (3E 430). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Slavery ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Black_Marsh
- **Slavery after abolition** — Abolition was enforced unevenly after the Red Year, and some Dunmer kept slaves in outlying areas. UESP's overall summary is that the decree "was apparently successful". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dunmer ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Slavery
- **Borders** — In 4E 4 the Morrowind–Black Marsh border is presumably hostile but trading. From 4E 5–6 it is a war front. [unverified]

## 5. Culture & daily life (food, drink, religion, naming conventions with examples, festivals, slang)
- **Self-name** — Argonians call themselves **Saxhleel**, "People of the Root", and many prefer it to "Argonian". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Argonian
- **Lukiul** — An Argonian assimilated into non-Argonian (e.g. Imperial) culture, typically one who has left the swamp. Some adopt a Cyrodiilic name. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Argonian ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/sharper-tongue-jel-primer
- **Ojel** — An outsider who does not speak Jel and does not belong to a tribe. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Jel ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/lang-jel
- **Jel language** — Has no tense system. Speakers mark past or future only when needed. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/lang-jel
- **Jel vocabulary** — *beeko* (friend), *deelith* (teacher), *greel* (enemy), *kaal* (war captain), *thtithil* (egg), *uxith* (nest/home/bed), *vastei* (change), *vakka* (sun). The list is fan-compiled from game texts and some glosses are uncertain. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/lang-jel ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Jel
- **Rank prefix** — *Raj-* means "elder", so *Raj-kaal* is an elder war captain, a high military rank. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Jel
- **Tribe suffix** — *-leel* or *-hleel* marks a people or tribe: Saxhleel, Veeskhleel (Ghost People of Murkmire), Mihuitleel (a feathered people of Solstice). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Jel
- **Naming: two names** — Argonians generally have a Jel name and a Tamrielic name. Native-born Argonians receive their Jel name after passing the **Chukka-Sei**. This is most likely what they give outsiders as their "Black Marsh name". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Argonian_Names
- **Naming: translation** — Tamrielic names often translate the Jel name. **Haj-Ei** becomes "Hides-His-Eyes" (*haj* ≈ hidden, *ei* = eye/eyes). Others: *Wasseek-Haleel* = "Bright-Throats". *Otumi-Naat* is described as a poetic rather than literal name. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Argonian_Names ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Jel
- **Naming examples (Jel-style, hyphenated)** — Keema-Mota, Mach-Loh, Haj-Ei, Brand-Shei. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Argonian_Names ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Brand-Shei
- **Naming examples (from memory)** — Talen-Jei, Keerava, Madesi, Shahvee, Derkeethus (Skyrim); "Wanders-the-Marsh"-style descriptive names. [unverified] — https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Argonian_Names
- **Name uncertainty** — The Jel dictionary marks *Ojei* as "possibly" meaning "No-Eyes". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Jel
- **Naming Day** — The sap a hatchling ingests at its Naming Day ceremony shapes its appearance. The claim that sap also decides gender is unconfirmed and disputed on the talk page. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Argonian ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore_talk:Argonian
- **Hatching** — Eggs are usually incubated in pools of Hist sap, and the Hist assists the hatching. In Shadowfen, the **Keepers of the Shell** tend the Hatching Pools. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hist ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hatching_Pools
- **Hatchling customs** — Teething hatchlings chew pacifiers of hardened sap. Hatchlings are fed licorice worms. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Argonian
- **Religion: Hist** — Argonians lick sap from Hist trunks in religious rites. Through the sap the Hist send visions. Large doses are dangerous. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hist
- **Religion: Sithis** — Argonians are one of the few cultures that openly revere Sithis. Their priests are called **Nisswo**. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Sithis
- **Sport** — *Teeba-enoo* is a traditional Argonian team sport played with at least one ball. "Teeba-Enai" as a name was not confirmed. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/lang-jel
- **Argonians in Morrowind (4E)** — Argonians continue to live inside Morrowind. Relations with their neighbours are unclear. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Argonian
- **Memory of slavery** — Revenge for "millennia of slavery and oppression" is one of the leading theories for the invasion. This is useful for tone, though motives are unconfirmed. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War
- **Food (general)** — No dedicated Argonian cuisine page was found. [unverified for any specific dish]
- **Festivals** — None found for the 4E. [unverified]

## 7. Game hooks (SUGGESTIONS ONLY — events, rumors, price effects, route changes, encounters, codex ideas)
- SUGGESTION: **4E 4 (calm year):** Lilmoth is open only to licensed traders. Make a "Lilmoth Trade License" a purchasable permit (not a good) that unlocks the single crowded foreign market district.
- SUGGESTION: **Competing rumors on the Red Year aftermath (4E 5):** "The Hist sent them" / "Thalmor gold paid for those spears" / "It's payback for the chains" / "Nobody knows why" (Adril Arano's line). Present each with its source in the codex.
- SUGGESTION: **Mournhold rumor pair (4E 5–6):** "Mournhold has fallen to the Saxhleel!" versus "Mournhold stands untouched." Settle neither, matching the canon CONFLICT.
- SUGGESTION: **Route changes:** from "the months following" the Red Year (pick a date in 4E 5), close or raise risk on the Black Marsh ↔ southern Morrowind routes. From mid-4E 6, add raid risk to routes through Telvanni lands. Lower the risk once "Redoran halts the advance" (late 4E 6, our choice).
- SUGGESTION: **Price effects:** Dunmer refugee flows (Telvanni matrons on ships like the *Pride of Tel Vos*) raise demand for passage, food and luxury goods in northern ports. Imperial goods collapse in Black Marsh after the colonies are sacked.
- SUGGESTION: **Goods list for the Black Marsh market:** washtongue powder (pottery), marsh herbs (from Gideon dealers), dragonthorn leaves, hardened-sap pacifiers (cheap curio). Sacred Hist sap is "never for sale to ojel" and shows up only as a refused-trade flavour event.
- SUGGESTION: **Slavery treatment:** never tradeable. Use it as codex and NPC backstory (freed Argonians in Morrowind, Archein "traitors", Dres slavers turned refugees), and as a reason Argonian merchants refuse Dunmer clients.
- SUGGESTION: **Encounter (4E 5):** a Dunmer foundling wrapped in a Telvanni blanket, left in a marsh village. A nod to Brand-Shei. Do not name him, and never make the child a trade object.
- SUGGESTION: **Codex entries:** "Saxhleel", "Lukiul and Ojel", "Two Names (Jel and Tamrielic)", "Chukka-Sei", "The Organism", "Thtachalxan", "Archeins", "The Imperial Commerce Road", "Rising Threat (and its author's fate)".
- SUGGESTION: **NPC names:** use translated Jel names in Tamrielic form (Hides-His-Eyes style) for lukiul traders and hyphenated Jel names (Keema-Mota, Mach-Loh style) for marsh-born ones. Add *Raj-kaal* as a title for invasion commanders.
- SUGGESTION: **Long Chronicle beats:** c. 4E 28 "the Argonian advance has stopped" (approximate), 4E 48 Umbriel destroys Lilmoth, Gideon and Stormhold (dates in CONFLICT), and 4E 150 the Solstheim raid.
