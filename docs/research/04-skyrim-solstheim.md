# Skyrim, Solstheim and the Dunmer Refugees (3E 427 – c. 4E 40)
_Researcher notes for Ashfall Ledger. Status tags: (no tag) = verified via cited source; [unverified]; [fan]; CONFLICT._

> Method note: compiled from ~44 earlier WebSearch runs (UESP / Imperial Library), plus 5 gap-filling searches
> (Solitude, Nord names, Sea of Ghosts, mead, early-4E High Kings). The search tool returns summaries of pages,
> not full page text, so "verified" means "a UESP/IL search result states it". Mirror URLs (en.m., content*.uesp.net,
> redirect names such as `Lore:Hale_the_Pious` → `Lore:People_H`, `Dragonborn:` → `Skyrim:`) have been normalised.
> Several items are dated only by 4E 201 game data; those are flagged "(4E 201 context)" and must not be
> back-projected into 4E 4–6 without care.
>
> **Biggest gaps:** the name of the High King of Skyrim in 4E 4–6 (UESP lists only an "Unknown High King" around
> 4E 16), the Jarls of Windhelm and Solitude in 4E 4–6, any documented fur trade, and the exact day of the eruption.

## 1. Timeline (dated facts, oldest → newest; focus 3E 427 – 4E 40)
- **Early 3rd century of the Third Era** — Thirsk mead hall founded by Hrothmund the Red and fellow Skaal on Lake Fjalding, south of Skaal Village. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Thirsk
- **Late Third Era (before 3E 427)** — Solstheim, "apparently forgotten", is rediscovered by Imperials and claimed as "Imperial territory" by building Fort Frostmoth. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- **3E 426** — Death of Remas Morvayn (possible father of Lleril Morvayn; parentage disputed, see §6). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Lleril_Morvayn
- **3E 427** — East Empire Company founds Raven Rock as an ebony-mining colony after finding a rich ebony deposit on Solstheim's southern edge; construction takes several months and ore is shipped to Windhelm immediately. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:History_of_Raven_Rock
- **3E 427** — Bloodmoon Prophecy fulfilled on Solstheim; Hircine's Hunt foiled by the Nerevarine. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- **3E 427 (game data)** — Ferry service: the Khajiit S'virr at Khuul (Vvardenfell) sails only to Fort Frostmoth (6 hours); Raven Rock lies ~2 hours from Fort Frostmoth. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Morrowind:Transport
- **3E 427 – 3E 433** — Early colonists fight spriggans, draugr and local Nords; Company money lets the town grow fast; stalhrim found nearby opens further trade. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock
- **3E 433** — "The money had run out at Raven Rock"; the Nords of Skyrim campaign to claim all of Solstheim and remove the Imperial fort. Solstheim is integrated into the province of Skyrim, but Fort Frostmoth stays garrisoned by the Legion (extent disputed, see §6). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- **3E 433** — Some Raven Rock colonists leave for Cyrodiil after the money runs out (several later turn up in the Imperial City). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock
- **3E 433 (Oblivion Crisis)** — Most Legion soldiers recalled to Cyrodiil; no record of any Oblivion Gate on Solstheim. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- **3E 433 (Oblivion Crisis)** — Ald'ruhn destroyed; this drives House Redoran's later move to Solstheim. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Brara_Morvayn
- **c. 3E 433 onward** — Dunmer emigration into Cyrodiil (mainly Cheydinhal) predates the Red Year. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dunmer
- **4E 1** — Great House Redoran sends an expedition under Brara Morvayn to settle at Raven Rock; she brokers a deal with the East Empire Company (rumours of bribes). Date disputed, see §6. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:History_of_Raven_Rock
- **Early 4E (before 4E 5, "presumably")** — Telvanni wizard Neloth founds Tel Mithryn on Solstheim's southeast coast. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Tel_Mithryn
- **4E 4** — Rieklings attack Raven Rock, killing six miners and the Company's Factor; Brara Morvayn and her Redoran men drive them off and the East Empire Company names her Imperial Factor of the settlement. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Brara_Morvayn ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Lleril_Morvayn
- **4E 5 (Red Year)** — Red Mountain erupts; Vvardenfell devastated; "this chain of events kills thousands". House Redoran takes charge of relief efforts and gains power among the Great Houses. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era
- **4E 5** — Fort Frostmoth almost completely destroyed by the eruption; most of the garrison, including General Falx Carius, killed by debris. Never rebuilt. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fort_Frostmoth ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Falx_Carius
- **4E 5** — Much of Raven Rock's population survives the blast by sheltering inside the mine. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era
- **4E 5** — With Frostmoth gone, elite Redoran Guard replace the remaining Imperial garrison at Raven Rock. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- **4E 5** — Volcanic debris strikes Solstheim's southern coast (craters, rubble, heart stones); Hirstaang Forest largely wiped out except a few groves on the edge of the Isinfier Plains. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim_Ashlands ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Hirstaang_Forest
- **4E 5** — Natural disasters in Morrowind send many Dunmer fleeing into eastern Skyrim; refugees say they kept walking west and Windhelm was the first city they reached. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Skyrim ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Elder_Scrolls_Historical_References
- **4E 5** — Black Marsh secedes from the Empire after the explosion of Red Mountain. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era
- **4E 5 (start)** — Accession War / Argonian invasion of Morrowind begins; thousands of Dunmer killed, many flee west to Skyrim "over land and sea". (Start date disputed, see §6.) — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War
- **c. 4E 5–6** — Ash storms repeatedly hit Raven Rock and halt mining; Brara designs a wall to shield the east end of town, the Company funds it, and after roughly a year of work it is finished and named **the Bulwark**; mining resumes uninterrupted. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/history-raven-rock-vol-ii ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- **c. 4E 6** — Great House Telvanni's last remaining stronghold sacked by the Argonian invasion force. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Telvanni
- **4E 6, Second Seed, Middas** — Date of Lymdrenn Tenvanni's journal, written as Argonians close on his family home; he names infant son Brandyl "sole living heir to House Telvanni". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Lymdrenn_Tenvanni's_Journal
- **4E 6** — Refugee ship *The Pride of Tel Vos* escapes the raiders and wrecks on a small island east of Winterhold (sinking date disputed, see §6). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Wreck_of_The_Pride_of_Tel_Vos
- **After 4E 5 ("for years afterward")** — Dunmer flee across Tamriel, many to Skyrim and Solstheim. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Red_Year
- **After 4E 5** — The Jarl of Winterhold takes in refugees, especially those who could contribute to the College of Winterhold. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Winterhold_(city)
- **4E 10** — Raven Rock Mine breaks into Bloodskal Barrow; Company investigator Gratian Caerellius and assistant Millius enter; draugr kill Gratian when he tries to take the Bloodskal Blade. The Company blames a tunnel collapse and seals that section (with its ebony). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Gratian_Caerellius
- **4E 16** — The High King of Skyrim formally gives Solstheim to the Dunmer (to Morrowind) as a refuge after the Red Year. UESP's ruler table lists him/her only as "Unknown High King". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Skyrim ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era
- **4E 16** — East Empire Company relinquishes Raven Rock to House Redoran; Imperial residents exodus to Cyrodiil; the Council names Brara Morvayn councilor (first councilor of Dunmer Raven Rock). — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/history-raven-rock ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock
- **4E 16 onward** — Redoran administer the whole island; Telvanni keep Tel Mithryn; Dunmer refugees, including minor House Sathil, "immediately began flocking to the island". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Hlaalu
- **4E 17** — (Imperial Library) Colovian warlord Titus Mede takes the Imperial City; "Skyrim endorses his ascension" (date disputed, see §6). — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era
- **4E 18** — Attrebus Mede, son of Titus Mede, born. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era
- **4E 20** — The Jarls of Skyrim issue the *Decree of Monument* making Refugees' Rest (east of Windhelm) a monument to the Dunmer who fled Morrowind after the Red Year. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Decree_of_Monument ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/decree-monument
- **c. 4E 22** — (UESP) Titus Mede captures the Imperial City with fewer than a thousand men, deposing Thules the Gibbering; Hierem's support makes the Elder Council accept him as liberator. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era_Major ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Titus_Mede_I
- **c./before 4E 48** — House Sathil present on Solstheim; relationship with Redoran unknown; gone by 4E 201. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- _Later context (beyond the main window):_
- **4E 65** — Brara Morvayn dies of old age; son Lleril, then in Blacklight, takes her place as councilor. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Brara_Morvayn
- **4E 95** — House Hlaalu coup to seize Solstheim from Redoran foiled; conspirators executed. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Hlaalu
- **4E 122** — Great Collapse casts most of Winterhold into the Sea of Ghosts after a year of storms and huge waves. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Great_Collapse
- **4E 130** — Bulwark threatens collapse; Councillor Morvayn pays for repairs personally. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/history-raven-rock
- **c. 4E 170 → 4E 181** — Raven Rock Mine shows signs of drying up; officially exhausted and shut by Lleril in 4E 181; town turns to hunting and fishing, Redoran neglect grows. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/history-raven-rock
- **4E 175** — White-Gold Concordat; High King Istlod must outlaw Blades agents in Skyrim. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Istlod
- **4E 183** — Hoag Stormcloak, Jarl of Windhelm, dies while Ulfric is imprisoned (date rests on the Prima guide). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:People_H ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore_talk:Ulfric_Stormcloak
- **4E 201** — Ulfric kills High King Torygg in the Blue Palace; Rieklings take Thirsk; Raven Rock Mine reopened after the sealed sections are cleared; undead Carius attacks Raven Rock and is destroyed. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Torygg ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Thirsk ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Redoran ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Falx_Carius

## 2. Places (status in 4E 4, 4E 5, 4E 6, and later if known)

### Raven Rock (Solstheim, south coast)
- Controlling faction / ruler: East Empire Company colony from 3E 427 under a Company **Factor**; Factor killed by Rieklings in 4E 4 and replaced by Brara Morvayn (a Redoran councilor serving as Imperial Factor). From 4E 16: House Redoran township, Brara Morvayn as councilor. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Brara_Morvayn ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/history-raven-rock
- Name: from the black colouring of the ebony deposits around it. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock
- Population: early residents mostly Imperials and Nords; Redoran Dunmer arrive under Brara (4E 1 per one source); Imperials leave for Cyrodiil after 4E 16. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock
- Economy & signature goods: ebony ore (shipped to Windhelm); stalhrim found nearby; after 4E 181, hunting and fishing. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:History_of_Raven_Rock ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock
- 4E 4: Riekling raid kills six miners and the Factor; Brara becomes Factor. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Brara_Morvayn
- 4E 5: townsfolk survive the eruption's blast inside the mine; Redoran Guard take over garrison duty from the Legion. — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- 4E 5–6: ash storms halt mining; the Bulwark is built (≈1 year, Company-funded, Brara-designed); without it the town "would have been buried beneath the ash". — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/history-raven-rock-vol-ii ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fort_Frostmoth
- 4E 10: mine breaks into Bloodskal Barrow; section sealed, death of Gratian Caerellius covered up as a cave-in. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Gratian_Caerellius
- Later: Bulwark later deteriorates, ash seeping through large cracks. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- Atmosphere details: black ebony-stained rock; a great wall of the Bulwark holding back ash drifts on the east side; Redoran guards in place of legionaries; miners living with the knowledge that the mine saved them in the Red Year. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era
- Sources: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:History_of_Raven_Rock ; https://www.imperial-library.info/content/history-raven-rock ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Bloodmoon:Raven_Rock

### Fort Frostmoth (Solstheim, south coast)
- Controlling faction / ruler: Imperial Legion; Captain (later General) Falx Carius. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Falx_Carius
- Economy: military outpost; 3E 427 ferry terminus from Khuul; Raven Rock ~2 hours away. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Morrowind:Transport
- 4E 4: still garrisoned by the Legion even after Skyrim's 3E 433 claim; Carius reportedly saw the Dunmer at Raven Rock as a threat and sent demands for their "surrender" (timing disputed). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- 4E 5: almost completely destroyed by the eruption (debris / shockwave); most of the garrison and Carius killed; never rebuilt. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fort_Frostmoth
- Later: ruins taken over by Ash Spawn and albino spiders; necromancer Ildari Sarothril (Neloth's apprentice) reanimates Carius with a Heart Stone; 4E 201 he is destroyed. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fort_Frostmoth ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ildari_Sarothril
- Atmosphere: shattered grey walls half-buried in ash, the last Imperial flag on the island gone. — [unverified; flavour only]
- Sources: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fort_Frostmoth ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Falx_Carius

### Solstheim (island overall) and the Solstheim Ashlands
- Controlling faction: nominally Skyrim from 3E 433 (Legion still at Frostmoth); ceded to the Dunmer/Morrowind in 4E 16; Redoran administer it thereafter. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- Skaal tradition: Solstheim was originally part of Skyrim, made into an island during a battle between Dragon Cult loyalists and rebels. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Skaal
- Hircine's hunting ground, to which he returns every era (Bloodmoon Prophecy). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- 4E 5: ashfall extends far into the Sea of Ghosts, covering the southern coast; within a few years the south becomes ashlands. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim_Ashlands
- Rivers Harstrad, Iggnir and Isild flow into the Sea of Ghosts. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Sea_of_Ghosts
- Ash Spawn: heart stones in the volcanic debris animate the bones of Solstheim's dead buried in the wastes, infused with Red Mountain's ash; immune to poison, highly fire-resistant. First-appearance date not given. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ash_Creature ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Bestiary_A
- Later: by 4E 201 continuing ashfall has poisoned the southern coast and is killing fish. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim

### Skaal Village (Felsaad Coast, north Solstheim)
- Controlling faction: the Skaal (Nord offshoot tribe); Skaal Honor Guards protect the village. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Skaal_Village
- Northernmost settlement in Tamriel; north of Thirsk. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Skaal_Village
- Economy: hunting and minor agriculture; stalhrim smithing is a Skaal art. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Skaal_Village ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:A_New_Source_of_Stalhrim
- 4E 4–6: no specific events recorded; north lies outside the ashlands. — [unverified for 4E 4–6 specifics]
- Atmosphere: small, sturdy wooden dwellings ringed around a central Greathall used for feasts and gatherings; six All-Maker Stones on the island. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Skaal_Village ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:All-Maker

### Thirsk Mead Hall (Lake Fjalding, Felsaad Coast)
- Founded by Hrothmund the Red and fellow Skaal, early 3rd century 3E. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Thirsk
- Hunters of Thirsk call mead "the nectar of Shor". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mead
- Later (c. 4E 201): under Bujold the Intrepid the hall turns to feasting over war; Rieklings drive the Nords out. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Thirsk ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Riekling
- Not connected to Baron Thirsk (a Grievous Twilight of Molag Bal) despite the name. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Thirsk

### Tel Mithryn (southeast Solstheim)
- Neloth's Telvanni settlement, founded "presumably before 4E 5"; cordial with Redoran, Raven Rock and Tel Mithryn "content to ignore each other". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Tel_Mithryn ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- Neloth's apprentice Ildari Sarothril later became the necromancer who raised Carius. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ildari_Sarothril

### Windhelm (Eastmarch) and the Snow Quarter → Gray Quarter
- Controlling faction / ruler: Stormcloak clan in later centuries (Hoag "Bear of Eastmarch", d. 4E 183; then Ulfric). Jarl in 4E 4–6 not found. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:People_H
- Economy: received Raven Rock's ebony from 3E 427; docks serve the Solstheim run (Northern Maiden, 4E 201 context). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:History_of_Raven_Rock ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Northern_Maiden
- 4E 5 onward: first city reached by refugees walking west; Dunmer relegated to the run-down **Snow Quarter**, later named the **Gray Quarter** after them; "constant suspicion from the xenophobic local Nords". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Windhelm
- Conditions (4E 201 context): cramped slum below the rest of the city; Dunmer not violently persecuted but Nords openly xenophobic; Ulfric turns a blind eye. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Windhelm
- Hoag (Ulfric's father) was "reportedly more tolerant" of Windhelm's Argonian and Dunmer populations. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:People_H
- Atmosphere (4E 201 context): New Gnisis Cornerclub tavern beside Sadri's Used Wares in the Gray Quarter. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:New_Gnisis_Cornerclub
- Sources: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Windhelm ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Windhelm ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dunmer_of_Skyrim

### Refugees' Rest and Dunmeth Pass (Eastmarch, Skyrim–Morrowind border)
- Dunmeth Pass: one of the few traversable roads through the Velothi Mountains between northwest Morrowind and northeast Skyrim. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dunmeth_Pass
- Refugees' Rest: small tower built by local Nords as a waypoint for safe passage from the east; the last building before the border on the road northeast of Windhelm. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dunmeth_Pass ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Refugees'_Rest
- 4E 5–6: refugees meet loved ones there or leave notices for those who did not survive. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dunmeth_Pass
- 4E 20: declared a monument by all the Jarls of Skyrim; plinth with the Decree of Monument. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Refugees'_Rest
- Later (4E 201): ruined, foundation collapsing, bones and rubble inside; old Dwemer tunnels under the mountains used by caravans and refugees from Morrowind. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Refugees'_Rest ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dunmeth_Pass

### Winterhold (city)
- After the Red Year, its Jarl took in refugees, preferring those useful to the College. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Winterhold_(city)
- Pride of Tel Vos wreck lies on an island east of Winterhold (SW of the Serpent Stone); bandit camp by 4E 201. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Wreck_of_The_Pride_of_Tel_Vos
- Later: 4E 122 Great Collapse; College blamed because it survived intact; the College says the cause was the Vivec earthquake and Red Mountain eruption. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Winterhold_(city) ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Great_Collapse

### Solitude (Haafingar)
- Blue Palace: seat of the Jarls of Solitude and of the High Kings of Skyrim. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Blue_Palace
- Ruler 4E 4–6: not found; the High King of the period is unnamed in UESP. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Skyrim
- East Empire Company Warehouse (4E 201 context): on the docks southeast of the city; Company headquarters and main warehouse in Skyrim, in a vast cavern under the mountain; gates once windmill-driven, now worked by dock labourers. Whether it already existed in 4E 4–6 is not stated. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:East_Empire_Company_Warehouse ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solitude
- Later: Elisif the Fair, Torygg's widow, rules from the Blue Palace (4E 201). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solitude
- City built on a great natural arch; Bards College; "Burning of King Olaf" festival. — [unverified] (should be on https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Great_Arch and https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solitude)
- Note: a UESP "East Empire Expansion" page about the Blue Palace is a mod. — [fan] (omitted)

### Sea of Ghosts (and islands)
- Extent: along Tamriel's northern shore, Morrowind's eastern coast and Atmora's southern coast; misty waters full of glaciers and icebergs. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Sea_of_Ghosts
- Name: from the legend of Ysgramor's eldest son Yngol, killed in a storm in the Atmoran Strait and "claimed by the Sea-Ghosts". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Sea_of_Ghosts
- Ice: permafrost-covered glacial islands and icebergs off the Northern Coast; the ice-crusted shore east of the Great Arch never thaws ("the Frozen Coast"). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Northern_Coast
- Atmora to the north is frozen in permanent winter (the "Frostfall"); Sea Giants are the nearest known people north of Skyrim. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Atmora ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Sea_of_Ghosts
- Bleakrock Isle lies between Windhelm and Solstheim, "far from major trading routes"; Horker Island lies east of Solstheim (treasure of Haknir Death-Brand). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Places_I
- Wildlife: horkers (walrus-like, packs of 3–4, aggressive when provoked or in mating season, fast in water); dreugh. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Horker ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Sea_of_Ghosts
- 4E 5: Red Mountain ashfall extends far into the Sea of Ghosts. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim_Ashlands
- Pirates (4E 201 context): the Blood Horkers raid without fear of retaliation from Empire or Windhelm. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Blood_Horker
- Routes: Khuul→Fort Frostmoth (6 h, 3E 427); Windhelm→Raven Rock (Northern Maiden, 4E 201). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Morrowind:Transport ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Northern_Maiden

## 3. Factions & people (who, stance, fate)

- **The Factor of Raven Rock (name not found)** — Company official killed in the 4E 4 Riekling raid. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Brara_Morvayn

## 4. Goods, trade & laws relevant to this area
- **Ebony (Raven Rock)** — the colony's reason to exist; ore shipped Raven Rock → Windhelm from 3E 427. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:History_of_Raven_Rock
- Ebony supply 4E 5: interrupted by ash storms until the Bulwark is finished (~1 year); then "uninterrupted". — Source: https://www.imperial-library.info/content/history-raven-rock-vol-ii
- Ebony supply 4E 10: Company seals productive sections after Bloodskal breach (hidden reserves). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- **Stalhrim** — enchanted ice found only on Solstheim; found near Raven Rock early on, "opened further trade". — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Stalhrim
- Stalhrim is holy to the Skaal; they once shunned its use; its forging is sacred and stalhrim smithing is known almost only to Skaal smiths. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Stalhrim ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:A_New_Source_of_Stalhrim
- Ancient Nords encased bodies in stalhrim (a privilege for Nords) and sealed tombs with it against necromancy; the practice fell out of favour. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Stalhrim
- **Mead** — Nord mead is fermented honey or molasses and water, some brewers adding grain mash. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mead
- Black-Briar Mead c. 2E 582 was exported as far as Ebonheart; its production was revived in the Fourth Era (start date not given) by the Black-Briar family of Riften, with rival Honningbrew of Whiterun eliminated by 4E 201. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mead
- Riften is "the burgeoning capital of mead-making in Skyrim"; blackberry mead is the Black-Briar style; "Reserve" is sometimes just regular mead rebottled. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mead ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Alcoholic_Beverages
- **Fish** — Solstheim's fishing ruined by Red Mountain ash (Gjalund quit fishing); southern coast poisoned by 4E 201. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Gjalund_Salt-Sage ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim
- **Furs / hides / horker products** — Skaal live by hunting; Raven Rock later lives by hunting and fishing; a horker-skin cloak appears in sailors' lore. No source documents a fur trade as such. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Skaal_Village ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Sea_of_Ghosts ; fur trade itself [unverified]
- **Heart stones** — volcanic rocks with heart stones fell on south Solstheim in 4E 5; they animate ash spawn and were used to reanimate Carius (necromantic, dangerous). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim_Ashlands ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Falx_Carius
- **Passenger / supply shipping** — Windhelm–Raven Rock ferry (250 gold fare in 4E 201 game data) for supplies and people. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Northern_Maiden
- **Refugee land routes** — Dunmeth Pass through the Velothi Mountains; Dwemer tunnels as smuggler/caravan routes (4E 201 context). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dunmeth_Pass
- **Laws** — after 4E 175 the Blades are outlawed in Skyrim (Concordat). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Istlod
- **Laws** — Gray Quarter is barely patrolled by the city guard, leaving Dunmer to "mete out whatever passes for justice in their native customs" (biased Nord source). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Scourge_of_the_Gray_Quarter
- **Currency** — Septims ("gold") as the imperial currency. — [unverified] (game data uses "gold": https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Northern_Maiden)
- **Contraband** — skooma and moon sugar contraband in Skyrim. — [unverified] (not covered by any search result)

## 5. Culture & daily life
- **Mead halls**: Nords formed communities around mead halls, which grew into settlements such as Whiterun. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mead
- **Mead etiquette**: offering mead can be a challenge or test, or a sign of respect. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mead
- **Afterlife**: Sovngarde is a huge mead hall housing the souls of honourable heroes, by will of Shor. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Mead
- **Nord given names**: in Arena/Daggerfall built from 13 prefixes + 13 suffixes; names are chosen from omens in a ceremony while the child is young. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Nord_Names
- **Nord surnames (old style)**: patronymic *-sen* ("son of"), used by men and women alike; feminine *-dottir* in some later sources. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Nord_Names ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Nord_Names/Family
- **Nord surnames (modern style)**: from Morrowind onward most Nords have no patronym and use compound/hyphenated names (Bearclaw, Ironhand, Battle-Born). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Nord_Names
- **Clan names vs. epithets**: Battle-Born, Gray-Mane, Black-Briar, Snow-Shod, Cruel-Sea, Shatter-Shield, Silver-Blood, Ice-Blade read as clan names; widely shared ones like One-Eye read as personal bynames (UESP editor argument, not in-game text). — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore_talk:Nord_Names
- **Name examples from this area**: Gjalund Salt-Sage, Frilgeth Horse-Breaker, Hrothmund the Red, Hoag Stormcloak "Bear of Eastmarch", Bujold the Intrepid/the Unworthy, Haknir Death-Brand, Elisif the Fair, Torygg, Istlod. — Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Gjalund_Salt-Sage ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Scourge_of_the_Gray_Quarter ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Thirsk ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:People_H ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Places_I ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Torygg

## 6. Conflicts & uncertainties
- CONFLICT: **When Brara's Redoran arrived at Raven Rock.** Lore:Solstheim says 4E 1 (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim). History of Raven Rock says only that she came after Ald'ruhn's fall, by a Company deal with rumoured bribes, before becoming Factor in 4E 4 (https://www.imperial-library.info/content/history-raven-rock). Some pages imply the group arrived in 4E 1 and only later won Company backing (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:History_of_Raven_Rock).
- CONFLICT: **When the East Empire Company left Solstheim.** Lore:Raven_Rock (as summarised) says the Company "had already withdrawn … judging the ebony mine unprofitable" before 4E 16 (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock), and Lore:Fort_Frostmoth says it withdrew "by this time" in the Red Year context (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fort_Frostmoth). History of Raven Rock says it was "forced to relinquish" Raven Rock in 4E 16 (https://www.imperial-library.info/content/history-raven-rock). The Company also funded the Bulwark after 4E 5 and sealed the mine in 4E 10 (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim), so it was clearly still present until at least 4E 10.
- CONFLICT: **Did Skyrim's 3E 433 claim succeed?** One UESP text says the campaign succeeded and Solstheim was integrated into Skyrim, with only Frostmoth still Imperial (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim). Another says Skyrim gained official control but the fort, Raven Rock and its mines stayed intact. A third says the Legion held the Nords off from the fort and Raven Rock (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock).
- CONFLICT: **Why the Dunmer fled.** Lore:Skyrim says natural disasters of the Red Year (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Skyrim). Lore:Dunmer and Lore:Accession_War say the Argonian invasion (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Dunmer ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War). Some pages also note Dunmer migration to Skyrim and Winterhold before the Red Year (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Windhelm).
- CONFLICT: **Motive for the 4E 16 cession.** It is presented as compassionate, but Lore:Solstheim says Skyrim could not refuse refugees without provoking Morrowind, which had long claimed the island (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim). The Imperial Library says it was "ceded by Skyrim" as a refuge, while the Morrowind page says the Nords "conceded" it (https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Morrowind). Sources also differ on whether it was given "to the Dunmer" or "to Morrowind".
- CONFLICT: **Start of the Argonian invasion.** Lore:Accession_War says it was fought "starting in 4E 5" (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War). Telvanni material dates the decisive sacking to c. 4E 6 (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:House_Telvanni).
- CONFLICT: **When the Pride of Tel Vos sank.** Lore:Accession_War says it wrecked in 4E 6 (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Accession_War). The wreck and journal pages give only the journal date (4E 6, Second Seed, Middas), not the sinking date (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Lymdrenn_Tenvanni's_Journal). Whether the ship's name implies Tel Vos itself fell is disputed (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Aryon ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore_talk:Aryon). The surname "Tenvanni" may be a misspelling of Telvanni.
- CONFLICT: **How Fort Frostmoth fell.** UESP says "almost completely destroyed" by falling debris (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fort_Frostmoth). The Imperial Library says a shockwave "levels" it (https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era).
- CONFLICT: **Carius's hostility to the Dunmer.** Lore:Solstheim says he sent "surrender" demands as a living general (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim). Lore:Falx_Carius says it is unclear whether he saw the Dunmer as invaders before his death or only after reanimation (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Falx_Carius).
- CONFLICT: **When Carius was reanimated.** The Carius page says "two centuries later" (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Falx_Carius). The Sarothril material says "some time before 4E 201". Sources also differ on whether Ildari simply left him or sealed him in the fort (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Ildari_Sarothril).
- CONFLICT: **Raven Rock Mine's depletion.** The book says the veins were exhausted by 4E 181 (https://www.imperial-library.info/content/history-raven-rock). Lore:Solstheim says ebony remained in the sections the Company sealed in 4E 10 (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim). The decline is dated c. 4E 170 in both UESP and the IL timeline.
- CONFLICT: **The Snow Quarter → Gray Quarter renaming.** Lore:Windhelm states it neutrally, named after the refugees (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Windhelm). Athal Sarys frames it as "the reality of the Dunmer occupation", a "victory" (https://www.imperial-library.info/content/dunmer-skyrim). Frilgeth frames the district as squalor and crime (https://www.imperial-library.info/content/scourge-gray-quarter). Both in-world books are partisan.
- CONFLICT: **Skaal hospitality.** Lore:Skaal_Village calls them traditionally unfriendly to outsiders (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Skaal_Village). *Children of the All-Maker* calls them "impressively hospitable" (https://www.imperial-library.info/content/children-all-maker).
- CONFLICT: **Skaal origin.** Scholars debate Atmoran origin versus a split from mainland Nords (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Skaal).
- CONFLICT: **Stalhrim use.** The Skaal "at one point shunned its usage", yet forging it is sacred to them and the craft is theirs (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Stalhrim). The etymology ("steel" + "rime") is only a talk-page theory (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim_talk:Stalhrim).
- CONFLICT: **Titus Mede's rise and Skyrim's endorsement.** The Imperial Library says 4E 17 with "Skyrim endorses his ascension", citing *Rising Threat* vol. 4 (https://www.imperial-library.info/content/fourth-era). UESP says c. 4E 22 (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Fourth_Era_Major). A UESP talk page reads *Rising Threat* as c. 4E 29 (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore_talk:Fourth_Era). The endorsement rests on one source.
- CONFLICT: **Hoag Stormcloak's death.** The 4E 183 date is sourced only to the Prima guide (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore_talk:Ulfric_Stormcloak). A Falkreath Hold page notes differing accounts of a Hoag's death place (Glenumbra Moors versus Falkreath) (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Falkreath_Hold), which likely concerns a different, Second Era Hoag [unverified].
- CONFLICT: **Lleril's parentage.** Sources disagree on whether Remas Morvayn (d. 3E 426) was his father (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Lleril_Morvayn).
- CONFLICT: **The 4E 130 Bulwark repair.** IL and UESP give slightly different accounts (https://www.imperial-library.info/content/history-raven-rock ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Solstheim).
- CONFLICT: **Brara's title.** Sources differ on whether she was only the Company's Factor (4E 4–16) or is also styled ruler of the island (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Brara_Morvayn ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Lleril_Morvayn).
- CONFLICT: **The Company's name.** Sources variously call it the East Empire Company, East Empire Trading Company or Imperial Trading Company (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:East_Empire_Company ; https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Raven_Rock).
- CONFLICT: **Cause of the Great Collapse (4E 122).** The Jarl's council blamed the College (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Deneth). The College blames the Vivec earthquake and Red Mountain (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Winterhold_(city)). UESP says the cause is unknown (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Great_Collapse).
- UNCERTAIN: The High King of Skyrim in 4E 4–6 and in 4E 16 is unnamed ("Unknown High King") (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Skyrim). The Jarls of Windhelm and Solitude in 4E 4–6 were not found.
- UNCERTAIN: The exact day of the Red Mountain eruption is not given in these results. [unverified]
- UNCERTAIN: Whether the Solitude East Empire Company Warehouse existed in 4E 4–6 is not stated (https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:East_Empire_Company_Warehouse).

## 7. Game hooks (SUGGESTIONS ONLY — not lore facts)
- SUGGESTION: **"Factor's Funeral" (4E 4).** A Riekling raid on Raven Rock closes the mine for some days and spikes ebony ore prices in Windhelm. A rumour says "a Redoran woman is the new Factor".
- SUGGESTION: **Red Year shock (4E 5).** The Khuul–Frostmoth ferry route is permanently deleted, and Raven Rock ebony output drops near zero until a "Bulwark completed" event about a year later. Selling timber or stone to Raven Rock then pays well.
- SUGGESTION: **Ash-fouled fishing.** Solstheim/Sea of Ghosts fish prices rise and supply falls after 4E 5. A codex entry can quote a Gjalund-type sailor (use as flavour, not as a dated fact).
- SUGGESTION: **Refugee wave.** Windhelm's Snow Quarter fills with Dunmer: cheap labour, more Dunmer goods (and resentment) for sale, and Nord rumour events drawn from *Scourge of the Gray Quarter* set against Dunmer rumours drawn from *The Dunmer of Skyrim*.
- SUGGESTION: **Dunmeth Pass caravan risk.** Spring 4E 5 onward: crowded roads, notices pinned at the waystation tower (later Refugees' Rest), and an encounter where a traveller asks the player to carry a message to a lost relative.
- SUGGESTION: **The Pride of Tel Vos (4E 6, Second Seed).** A rumour of a Telvanni refugee ship lost off Winterhold sets up a salvage event. Do not state the wreck date as fact; the sources disagree.
- SUGGESTION: **Stalhrim deals.** These need Skaal trust. The Skaal refuse to trade, or charge a premium, for something they hold holy, which makes stalhrim a "sacred / taboo" commodity.
- SUGGESTION: **Rumour pair for the CONFLICT entries.** "The Company is pulling out of Solstheim" set against "The Company just paid for a giant ash wall". Also "Skyrim owns Solstheim" set against "The Legion still holds Frostmoth".
- SUGGESTION: **Heart stones** as a black-market curiosity after 4E 5, with a necromancy flag and guard confiscation risk. The item is canon; any trade in it is our invention.
- SUGGESTION: **Long Chronicle beats.** 4E 10 (rumour of a mine "cave-in" and a dead Imperial investigator), 4E 16 (Raven Rock flips to Redoran, Imperial merchants leave, tariffs change), 4E 17/22 (Titus Mede rumours with contradictory dates), 4E 20 (Decree of Monument codex unlock).
- SUGGESTION: **Mead market.** Nord mead in general is safe to sell in Skyrim taverns. Avoid tying Black-Briar or Honningbrew to 4E 4–6 because their Fourth Era revival date is unknown. Use a mead-offer "challenge or respect" dialogue mechanic.
- SUGGESTION: **Sea of Ghosts hazards.** Iceberg and fog events, horker-pack and dreugh encounters. Bleakrock Isle is a quiet, off-route smuggler stop.
