_The researched lore behind the game, organised by topic. Every fact carries a source link.

## 0. How to read this file

| Tag | Meaning | In-game badge |
|---|---|---|
| _(none)_ | Supported by a UESP Lore / Imperial Library source (via search result) | — |
| `[unverified]` | From researcher memory; not confirmed by a search result | "Unverified" |
| `CONFLICT` / `D-xx` | Sources disagree; both sides recorded (see §2, Dispute Register) | "Historians disagree" |
| `[extrapolation]` | Our reasoned inference from canon (e.g. a plausible day for a year-only event) | "Extrapolation" |
| `[invented]` | Made up for gameplay (merchants, lender, counting-houses…) | "Invented for this game" |
| `[fan]` | Fan project content — **excluded** from the game | — |

**Method.** Direct page fetches to uesp.net / imperial-library.info were blocked in the build environment,
so ~240 WebSearch queries were run against those sites; a fact counts as sourced when a search result from
UESP/IL states it. Mirror URLs are normalised to `https://en.uesp.net/wiki/…`. When sources disagree we keep
both and use the current UESP subject article as the world simulation's **default reading**.

**In-universe bias.** Several key sources are in-world books with authors: *The Red Year* (Melis Ravel,
patriotic Dunmer oral history), *Rising Threat* (Lathenil of Sunhold, anti-Thalmor exile), *The Great War*
(Legate Justianus Quintius, Imperial soldier), *The Dunmer of Skyrim* (Athal Sarys, pro-Dunmer) vs *Scourge
of the Gray Quarter* (Frilgeth Horse-Breaker, anti-Dunmer). The codex names the voice when it quotes them.

## 1. Master timeline

### 1.1 Background (rumour and codex material)
- **3E 427** — Dagoth Ur destroyed; Almalexia kills Sotha Sil, then dies; the Nerevarine vanishes (rumoured to Akavir). [uesp:Almalexia] [uesp:Pocket_Guide_Morrowind] [uesp:Nerevarine]
- **3E 427** — Hlaalu Helseth becomes King of Morrowind (end of reign unrecorded). [uesp:Mournhold]
- **3E 427** — East Empire Company founds **Raven Rock** on Solstheim to mine ebony, shipped to Windhelm. [uesp:History_of_Raven_Rock]
- **3E 427 or 3E 433** — Vivec stripped of divinity and gone; fate disputed (**D-04**). [uesp:Vivec] [uesp-sk:Mask_of_Vivec]
- **3E 430** — Helseth abolishes slavery in Morrowind (cause and date disputed, **D-11**). [uesp:Slavery] [uesp:Hlaalu_Helseth]
- **3E 433** — Oblivion Crisis; Mythic Dawn kill Uriel VII and heirs; Martin Septim becomes the Avatar of Akatosh; the Septim line ends. [uesp:Oblivion_Crisis] [uesp:Martin_Septim]
- **3E 433** — Daedra destroy Ald'ruhn (Redoran's crab-shell city). [uesp:Ald'ruhn]
- **3E 433** — Kvatch ruined. [uesp-ob:Kvatch_Castle]
- **3E 433** — The Hist recall and alter the Argonians, who storm the Oblivion Gates until the Daedra close them; the An-Xileel claim the credit (**D-13**). [uesp:Argonian] [uesp:An-Xileel]
- **3E 433** — Summerset's Crystal Tower collapses; the Thalmor gain prestige as "saviours". [uesp:The_Great_War_(book)] [uesp:Thalmor]
- **After 3E 433** — The An-Xileel unite most Argonians, sack the Imperial colonies and rename Black Marsh **Argonia** (secession timing disputed, **D-06**). [uesp:Kingdom_of_Black_Marsh] [uesp:An-Xileel]
- **4E 1** — The Elder Council invokes its Charter; High Chancellor **Ocato** "reluctantly" becomes **Potentate** "until imperial rule was reestablished". [uesp:Fourth_Era] [uesp:Elder_Council]
- **4E 1 (disputed)** — Brara Morvayn leads Redoran refugees from Ald'ruhn to Raven Rock (**D-17**). [uesp:Solstheim] [il:History_of_Raven_Rock]
- **Before 4E 5** — With Vivec gone, the mage Vuhon (with Sul) builds the soul-fed **Ingenium** to hold the moonlet **Baar Dau** (the Ministry of Truth) aloft over Vivec City. [uesp:Baar_Dau] [uesp:Ingenium]

## 3. Places (game locations)

> Game rules, not lore: after the eruption Vivec City, Ebonheart and Fort Frostmoth stay on the map as **survivors' camps** where a few merchants still trade among the ruins `[extrapolation]`. Day-labour jobs in every place (`src/data/jobs.ts`) are `[invented]`, built on each town's canon trades.
Status codes used in data: `thriving · uneasy · ruined · rebuilding · besieged · sacked · occupied · refugee-swollen · closed`.

### 3.1 Vvardenfell
**Balmora** _(start; Hlaalu; Camonna Tong base)_
- Hlaalu council town on the Odai River, which divides it into four districts. [uesp:Odai_River] [uesp-mw:Balmora]
- Camonna Tong base (with Gnaar Mok); silt-strider port to Ald'ruhn, Seyda Neen, Suran, Vivec; Mages Guild Guide. [uesp:Camonna_Tong] [uesp-mw:Transport]
- The Council Club is the Camonna Tong's Balmora tavern. `[unverified]` (TES III)
- 4E 5: "hardly anything left standing"; relief c. a month later; rebuilt and re-inhabited. [uesp:Balmora]
- Game: `thriving` → `ruined` (3 SD 4E 5) → `rebuilding` (c. Midyear 4E 5) → `uneasy`.

**Vivec City** _(Temple seat; Baar Dau overhead)_
- Temple city under the hovering Ministry of Truth; a guar caravan road ran south to Narsis. [uesp:Baar_Dau] [il:Red_Year_Vol_II]
- 4E 5: obliterated; site becomes the boiling crater **Scathing Bay**; later "an island ruin of shattered stone and ash". [uesp:Red_Year] [uesp:Vivec_City]
- Witnesses recall "a sudden, unnatural silence", guar going wild, then a blast and a pillar of fire. [uesp:The_Red_Year,_Vol._II]
- Game: `uneasy` → `closed` (crater). Argonians later hold the Scathing Bay area (by 4E 48). [uesp:Vivec_City]

**Ald'ruhn** _(Redoran; built in the shell of the Emperor Crab Skar)_
- [uesp:Ald'ruhn] [uesp:Emperor_Crab]. Destroyed by Daedra in 3E 433, then "further devastation" in 4E 5. [uesp:Ald'ruhn]
- Game: `rebuilding` (from 3E 433) → `ruined`.

**Sadrith Mora** _(Telvanni council town, Zafirbel Bay; Imperial Wolverine Hall nearby)_
- [uesp:Sadrith_Mora] [uesp:Wolverine_Hall]. Telvanni councilors thinned by Arch-Mage Trebonius's death order (3E 427). [uesp:Sadrith_Mora]
- 4E 5: "widespread destruction", extent unknown; 4E 6: Telvanni lands overrun. [uesp:Red_Year] [uesp:Accession_War]
- Game: `thriving` → `ruined` (lighter than Balmora) → `besieged` → `sacked` (mid-4E 6, **D-07**).

**Ebonheart** _(Imperial administrative seat of the Vvardenfell District; Castle Ebonheart)_
- Duke Vedam Dren, former Hlaalu Grandmaster, ruled the District in 3E 427; 4E fate unknown. [uesp:Vedam_Dren]
- East Empire Company presence `[unverified]`. Destroyed with Vivec City — **inference only**. [uesp:Castle_Ebonheart]
- Game: `thriving` → `closed` (`[extrapolation]`).

### 3.2 Mainland Morrowind
**Mournhold** _(royal capital, Deshaan)_ — Azura cleared its ash storms after Almalexia's death. [uesp:Mournhold] 4E 5 spared; relief hub. 4E 6 sacked (**D-02**); palace empty thereafter; reclaimed by 4E 201. [uesp:Mournhold] Game: `thriving` → `refugee-swollen` → `sacked` → `occupied`.

**Blacklight** _(Redoran capital, north-west)_ — becomes Morrowind's capital; Grand Council in the Rootspire; later a Temple of Azura serves as hospice. [uesp:Blacklight] [il:Red_Year_Vol_I] Game: `uneasy` → `refugee-swollen` → capital (`thriving`, arms demand).

**Necrom** _(City of the Dead, east coast)_ — centre of Dunmer ancestor worship; ruler disputed (**D-21**); 4E 6 role unknown. [uesp:Necrom] Game: pilgrim trade; `besieged` risk late 4E 6 `[extrapolation]`.

**Tear** _(House Dres, south; historic slave market)_ — partly sinks into the swamp in 4E 5; in the invasion's path, fate unknown. [uesp:Tear] [uesp:House_Dres] Game: `uneasy` → `ruined` (flooded) → `occupied` (`[extrapolation]`, fate unknown).

**Narsis** _(House Hlaalu capital, south-west)_ — refuge for Vivec survivors on the south road (Saldus Llervu); "it is unknown what happened to" it after Hlaalu's fall. [uesp:Narsis] [il:Red_Year_Vol_II] Game: Hlaalu counting-house seat; the Tong's refuge after the eruption (`[invented]`); erratic prices after the expulsion.

### 3.3 Solstheim
**Raven Rock** — EEC ebony colony (3E 427), named for its black ebony-stained rock; stalhrim found nearby. [uesp:Raven_Rock] [uesp:History_of_Raven_Rock] 4E 4 Riekling raid; 4E 5 survives in the mine; Bulwark built; 4E 16 Redoran. Game: `thriving` → `ruined` (mine closed) → `rebuilding` (Bulwark) → `thriving`.

**Fort Frostmoth** — Legion fort; 3E 427 ferry from Khuul (6 h); Raven Rock ~2 h away. [uesp-mw:Transport] Destroyed 4E 5; ruins later haunted by ash spawn. [uesp:Fort_Frostmoth] Game: Legion quartermaster market → `closed`.

### 3.4 Skyrim
**Windhelm** — receives Raven Rock's ebony; first city refugees reach; Dunmer put in the run-down Snow Quarter, renamed the Gray Quarter, "constant suspicion from the xenophobic local Nords". [uesp:History_of_Raven_Rock] [uesp:Windhelm] Jarl in 4E 4–6 unknown. Game: `thriving` → `refugee-swollen`.

**Solitude** — Blue Palace, seat of Jarls and High Kings. [uesp:Blue_Palace] High King in 4E 4–16 unnamed ("Unknown High King"). [uesp:Skyrim] EEC warehouse attested only for 4E 201. Game: Imperial-aligned northern port.

### 3.5 Cyrodiil
**Imperial City** — seat of Ocato's regency (no Emperor); Market District "one of the best across the province"; Waterfront port and haunt of the Thieves Guild; Temple of the One with the stone Avatar of Akatosh. [uesp:Imperial_City] [uesp:Temple_of_the_One] [uesp:Fourth_Era]

**Cheydinhal** — Count **Andel Indarys**, ex-Hlaalu Dunmer, the only non-Imperial count in 3E 433; "heavy Dunmer influence" in its architecture; main destination of Dunmer emigrants. [uesp:Andel_Indarys] [uesp:Cheydinhal] [uesp:Dunmer] Game: `refugee-swollen` after 4E 5.

**Leyawiin** — House Caro (Count Marius, Countess Alessia, hostile to Argonians, Khajiit and Dunmer); estuary port on Topal Bay at the end of the Green Road; large Khajiit and Argonian population. [uesp:Leyawiin] [uesp:Marius_Caro] [uesp:Alessia_Caro] [uesp:Green_Road] Game: border customs grow harsher as Black Marsh closes.

### 3.6 Black Marsh (Argonia)
**Lilmoth** — port on Oliis Bay; seat of the An-Xileel's governing body **the Organism**; **only licensed foreigners may enter** and all foreign trade is crammed into one market district; guarded by the **Thtachalxan** ("drykillers"). [uesp:Lilmoth] [il:Infernal_City_Black_Marsh] Game: requires a **trade licence** (permit, not a good).

**Stormhold** — river port in the north; Second Era Pact capital of Black Marsh; nearest city to Morrowind. [uesp:Stormhold] 4E 4–6 role unrecorded; plausible staging point `[unverified]`.

### 3.7 Elsweyr
**Senchal** — one of the largest ports of southern Tamriel; haven for pirates, thieves and the black market; once the drug trade's chief port, it turned resort for wealthy Khajiit; the slum of **Black Kiergo** is lined with moon-sugar dens. [uesp:Senchal] [uesp:Black_Kiergo] Elsweyr nominally Imperial in 4E 4–6; de facto independent from c. 4E 10. [uesp:Elsweyr_Confederacy]

## 4. Factions & people

- **House Redoran** — warrior House; runs Red Year relief; halts the Argonians; moves capital to Blacklight; Hlaalu blame it for their expulsion. [uesp:House_Redoran] [uesp:House_Hlaalu]
- **House Hlaalu** — pro-Imperial merchant House, scapegoated after the Crisis, stripped of rank after the Red Year; later plots on Solstheim (4E 95). [uesp:House_Hlaalu]
- **House Sadras** — obscure House, "prior status and holdings unknown", fills the fifth seat. [uesp:House_Sadras]
- **House Telvanni** — wizard House of the north-east; nearly destroyed 4E 6 (**D-07**); Neloth survives at Tel Mithryn. [uesp:Accession_War] [uesp:Neloth]
- **House Indoril / House Dres** — Indoril orthodox and Temple-aligned; Dres the southern slaver-planters, bound to Hlaalu by Helseth's marriage to Lady Vendama (3E 430). [uesp:Slavery] [uesp:House_Dres]
- **Tribunal Temple → New Temple** — Ordinators disbanded and persecuted; former Dissident Priests demote the Tribunes to "saints" and venerate the Reclamations (Azura, Boethiah, Mephala); Ashlanders "largely vindicated". [uesp:Ordinators] [uesp:Dissident_Priests] [uesp:The_Reclamations] [uesp:Ashlanders]
- **Camonna Tong** — native Dunmer syndicate; outlanders "fit only for slavery"; smuggling, kidnapping; controlled Hlaalu councilors under Orvas Dren; 4E survival unknown. [uesp:Camonna_Tong]
- **East Empire Company** — chartered by the Emperor; **exclusive rights to raw ebony, raw glass, stalhrim and Dwemer artefacts** in Morrowind/Solstheim; "unclear dealings with the Thalmor" in the 4E; who holds its charter with no Emperor is unrecorded. [uesp:East_Empire_Company]
- **Imperial Legion** — at Fort Frostmoth under Carius until 4E 5; recall from Morrowind disputed (**D-15**). [uesp:Falx_Carius]
- **Elder Council / Ocato of Firsthold** — Altmer Potentate from 4E 1; took the Thalmor more seriously than most. [uesp:Ocato_of_Firsthold]
- **An-Xileel** — Argonian nativist party; "the Organism" in Lilmoth; launches the invasion; hold broken by Umbriel in 4E 48. [uesp:An-Xileel]

## 5. Goods, trade & law

### 5.1 Trade law
- Outside East Empire Company channels, the Empire bans dealing in **moon sugar, skooma and Dwemer artefacts** on Vvardenfell; smuggling is big business. [uesp:Vvardenfell]
- Morrowind law bans **moon sugar and skooma**; in practice possession goes unreported but most merchants refuse to trade with a carrier — **Khajiit merchants buy it**. [uesp-mw:Crime] [uesp-mw:Moon_Sugar]
- Dwemer artefacts are "property of the Emperor"; ebony trade restricted by Imperial decree. [uesp-mw:Crime]
- Moon sugar is smuggled through **Bitter Coast caves** and used as currency among bandits. [uesp:Moon_Sugar] [uesp-mw:Moon_Sugar]
- Skooma is banned by both the Third Empire and the Aldmeri Dominion; spoiled-sugar skooma is cheap and can kill. [uesp:Skooma]
- Talos worship is legal throughout this period (outlawed only in 4E 175). [uesp:Blades]
- No source gives tariff rates for 4E 4–6 → all duties in the game are `[invented]`.

### 5.2 Currency & credit
- Gold coins are called **septims** or **drakes** from the Third Empire's markings: Tiber Septim's face and the Red Dragon; the names persist into the Fourth Era. [uesp:Currency]
- Banks can issue loans and letters of credit called a **"writ of coin"**. [uesp:Currency]
- Daggerfall-era Iliac Bay banks offered deposits, deadline loans and letters of credit. `[unverified]` [uesp-df:Banks]

## 8. Culture & naming (drives the name generator)
- **Dunmer**: given name + family/House name (Brara Morvayn, Neria Relethyl, Saldus Llervu, Deros Dran, Melis Ravel, Adril Arano, Gilvas Barelo); Great House rulers may put the House first (Hlaalu Helseth). [uesp:Hlaalu_Helseth] [uesp:The_Red_Year,_Vol._I] Honorifics *sera, serjo, muthsera*, insult *n'wah* `[unverified]`.
- **Argonian**: a Jel name given after the **Chukka-Sei** rite plus a Tamrielic name, often a translation (Haj-Ei → "Hides-His-Eyes"); hyphenated Jel names (Keema-Mota, Mach-Loh); *Raj-* = elder; *lukiul* = assimilated, *ojel* = outsider. [uesp:Argonian_Names] [uesp:Jel] [uesp:Argonian]
- **Khajiit**: one honorific per name — *Ri'* (great leaders), *Ra'* (esteemed leaders), *Dar* (clever hands; may be a suffix), *Do'* (warriors), *Jo'* (scholars, mages — or mountebanks), *Ma'/M'* (child/apprentice), *J'* (young adult), *S'* (adult), *-dro/-dra* (elders); speech in third person ("this one… yes?"). Meanings rest largely on a 2001 developer piece. [uesp:Khajiit_Names] [il:Interview_With_Three_Booksellers] [uesp:Ta'agra]
- **Nord**: modern style is given name + clan or earned byname (Gjalund Salt-Sage, Frilgeth Horse-Breaker, Hoag Stormcloak); older *-sen* patronymics. [uesp:Nord_Names] [uesp-sk:Gjalund_Salt-Sage]
- **Imperial**: Roman-flavoured given + family names (Titus Mede, Marius Caro, Savlian Matius, Tertius Ione). [uesp:Imperial] [uesp:Titus_Mede_I]
- **Altmer**: single flowing names, place epithets ("of Firsthold"); Arena/Daggerfall prefix-suffix tables. [uesp:Altmer_Names]
- **Bosmer**: prefix-suffix given names; nature surnames (Fernwood) or clan bynames. [uesp:Bosmer_Names]
- **Redguard**: unisex, no surnames; *al-* "comes from", *at-* "descendant of". [uesp:Redguard_Names]
- **Orc**: *gro-* (male) / *gra-* (female) + clan or stronghold. [uesp:Orc_Names]
- **Daily life**: kwama eggs and saltrice porridge with scuttle; sujamma, greef, mazte [uesp:Dunmer_Cuisine] [uesp:Alcoholic_Beverages]; temple healing paid by "suggested donation" [uesp-df:Holidays]; Dunmer pilgrims now consult Ashlander Wise Women [uesp:Ashlanders]; the Empire's "ineffectual protection and seeming indifference" made it reviled by the Dunmer [uesp:Morrowind].
